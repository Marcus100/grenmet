# Design audit: GMS public weather site, the `gms` app (2026-11)

**Status:** Phase 1 audit, read only. Nothing was changed in the app; every fix below is a proposal for the owner.  
**Owner:** Barrels Grenada engineering (design mastery programme, issue #1453, section 8)  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-23

## Scope and method

Surfaces audited, all in `apps/web/gms` (the public weather site, `weather.staging.barrels.gd` on staging and `weather.barrels.gd` in production):

- the shared frame: masthead, desktop menu, phone menu, site search, breadcrumbs, footer, the loading, error and not-found pages;
- the home page and the dated weather routes (sky hero, day tabs, From the Desk, Weather now, Weather news, Grenada in data, Questions, Discover);
- the standing pages under Alerts, Weather, Marine, Climate, Services, Learn & Explore and About, with their shared building blocks (page header and section, information table, link list, placeholder notice, frequently asked questions, quiz);
- the three forms (alerts by email, newsletter, climate data request) and the browser notifications control, read and looked at but never submitted.

The rule for judging: a defect is a gap against this app's own lane spec ([`docs/design/gms.md`](../gms.md)), an owner rule recorded in the instructions, or a rule from a design skill, never a difference from another app.

Method:

1. Read the instructions (`AGENTS.md` at the root, in `apps/web/gms` and in `packages/gms`), the lane spec, and the section "Reviewing or polishing UI: check these before reporting a gap" in `docs/agents/extended-rules.md`.
2. Checked where styles live before reporting anything. The shared layer `packages/ui/src/styles/app-feel.css` is imported by the app's stylesheet (`src/app/globals.css`) and already switches off the tap flash, sets `touch-action: manipulation` on controls, stops long presses selecting button labels, contains scrolling inside dialogs and menus, and cancels CSS animation for people who ask for less motion. The app is wrapped in a motion provider that follows the same preference. Hover styles are written with Tailwind's `hover:` variant, which in this Tailwind version only applies on devices that can hover, so there is no stuck hover on phones. Nothing in this audit repeats a gap those files already cover.
3. Read the shared components and every page type listed above, then applied the rules of these skills: ui-polish-pass (states, widths, touch, slow connection, token discipline), web-design-guidelines (pinned rules), fixing-accessibility, mobile-native, emil-design-eng, break-ui (worst-case content, argued from the code), review-animations and the repository's refactoring-ui.
4. Looked at the running staging site with a scripted browser at 390 pixels (phone, touch emulation) and 1280 pixels (desktop) on 47 public pages, in light mode on all of them and in dark mode on eight; three pages were also loaded at 768 pixels (tablet). On every page the script measured tap target sizes, text under 12.5 pixels, sideways overflow, heading order and console errors. A second pass opened the phone menu, the search dialog and the desktop menu, walked the keyboard through the masthead, switched units and theme with the real buttons, loaded four pages on a throttled connection (about 1.6 megabits per second with 400 milliseconds of delay) and ran the axe accessibility checker on eight pages in both themes. Nothing was submitted. The screenshots are kept outside the repository and are not committed.
5. Left out everything already listed in issue #1688 (the 22 November staging audits and their tracker): the empty five-day strip, the stale current reading, the length and load time of the phone home page, the clipped observations table, the plain 404 page and missing `/search`, the empty marine, tropical outlook and radar products, the tides wording, and the tap targets and heading levels fixed in pull request #1732. Where this audit touches the same area it only reports what is still there after those fixes.

How to read a finding:

- **Rule or reference** is the lane spec line, owner rule or skill rule behind it.
- **Size:** S is under half a day, M is about a day, L is several days or needs a decision first.
- **INVISIBLE** means the public would not see a difference in normal use (accessibility, focus, keyboard, hidden hit areas, behaviour on failure). These can merge on green checks. **VISIBLE** means layout, colour or wording changes, so the owner reviews before and after screenshots first.

Owner rules respected throughout: no finding proposes hiding an empty section, taking a public page out of search engines, adding impact text to forecasts, changing the metric default, or showing GMS alerts in any colour but green.

## What is already strong

- **Honest states everywhere.** A feed that cannot be read says so and is never shown as an all-clear; a product that is not issued yet keeps its page and says "Not yet available from GMS"; an old reading is labelled "Out of date" with its age; figures not reported yet say "Not reported yet". No sample figure was found on any page.
- **The alerts page is the best page on the site.** One status wording everywhere, the level always in words as well as colour, both checks listed when nothing is in effect, warnings that ended in the last 24 hours shown as an explicit all-clear, and warnings never waiting on the slower outlook.
- **The automated accessibility check found nothing.** axe reported no failures on eight pages in light and in dark mode (home, alerts, observations, get alerts, monthly climate, quiz, radar, sitemap). Every image has alternative text, there is a skip link, a page language and one main landmark.
- **No sideways scrolling.** None of the 47 pages overflowed at 390 pixels. Tables turn into label and value cards on phones and scroll inside a focusable region on wider screens.
- **The phone menu handles the keyboard properly.** Focus moves into the menu, the page behind is made inert (no tab stop reached it in 80 presses), Escape closes it and focus returns to the menu button. Rows are 44 pixels or taller.
- **Dark mode is disciplined.** The rule "an ink used as a fill gets dark text in dark mode" is followed on every blue-ink and sky-ink fill, hazard colours keep their meaning, and the theme and units choices persist between pages.
- **Forms get the basics right.** Visible labels, 16 pixel text in text inputs (no zoom on iPhones), correct `type`, `inputMode` and `autoComplete`, a spam trap, a confirmation step by email, and the button label changes to "Sending…" while it cannot be pressed twice.
- **Third-party media waits for a tap.** Nothing loads from YouTube, Facebook or SoundCloud until the reader presses Play, and focus moves to the player.
- **Motion is restrained.** A 150 millisecond fade on page change, a 150 millisecond menu fade and a 200 millisecond desktop menu; no `transition-all`, nothing slower than 300 milliseconds, nothing on warning surfaces. The review-animations rules found nothing to block.
- **Fast on staging.** Standing pages loaded in 0.6 to 0.9 seconds and the home page in about 1.3 seconds on a normal connection; no console errors on any real page.

## Findings

### Blocking

#### 1. The units switch exists only on the home hero, so most pages cannot be switched

- **Where:** `UnitsToggle` is used once, inside the sky hero card (`src/components/home/sky-hero.tsx` lines 184 to 188). The masthead (`src/components/header.tsx`) and the phone menu (`src/components/nav-drawer.tsx`) do not have it. Measurements that follow the switch appear on standing pages too: observations (through `src/components/pages/info-table.tsx`), heat, tides, sea conditions and the hurricane season page.
- **What happens:** on staging, switching to imperial on the home page and opening `/weather/observations` showed the reading in imperial with no control on the page to change it back (the script counted zero unit buttons there). A visitor who lands on a standing page from a search engine has no way to change units at all.
- **Rule or reference:** owner rule "metric units by default with a header switch". web-design-guidelines: a setting that changes the whole site belongs where it is always reachable.
- **A second, smaller problem in the same control:** in the hero it is the bare text "°C" with no border or fill (measured: transparent background, transparent border), so it reads as a label for the big number, not a button.
- **Suggested fix:** put the switch in the masthead beside the theme button (and in the phone menu if the masthead has no room at 390 pixels), give it a visible button shape, and keep or drop the copy in the hero as the owner prefers. Size S to M. VISIBLE.

#### 2. The three forms can send personal details into the page address if the button is pressed before the page is ready

- **Where:** `src/components/pages/data-request-form.tsx` (line 101), `src/components/pages/newsletter-signup.tsx` (line 90) and `src/components/pages/alert-email-signup.tsx` (line 100). Each form has only an `onSubmit` handler, no `action` and no `method`, and its button is enabled in the first HTML the server sends.
- **What happens:** until the page's script has loaded, pressing the button makes the browser submit the form the old way, as a GET to the same page. For the data request that puts the name, email address, organisation and purpose into the address bar, the browser history and the server's request log, and nothing is actually sent to GMS. On a slow phone connection that window is several seconds. This was reasoned from the code; it was not tried, because nothing was to be submitted.
- **Rule or reference:** `docs/agents/extended-rules.md`, Form patterns: "A page-level form that only has `onSubmit` falls back to a native GET before hydration, which puts passwords and personal data in the URL. Disable its submit until hydrated." The auth and bookings apps already do this with `useHydrated()`; this app has no such guard (no match for `useHydrated` in `apps/web/gms/src`).
- **Suggested fix:** keep the submit button disabled until the page is ready, as the other apps do, and add `method="post"` as a second guard. Size S. INVISIBLE.

### Should fix

#### 3. In dark mode the "Official briefing" badge is navy text on a navy fill

- **Where:** `src/components/home/live-sections.tsx` lines 253 and 427: `bg-gm-navy text-gm-text-inverse … dark:text-gm-navy`.
- **What happens:** measured on staging in dark mode: text colour and background colour are both `rgb(11, 19, 43)`. The badge on the first From the Desk card disappears. The checker did not catch it because identical colours give it nothing to compare.
- **Rule or reference:** lane spec, Do's: "an ink used as a fill behind white text lightens in dark mode, so give it `dark:text-gm-navy`". That rule is for the blue-ink and sky-ink fills; navy fills "do not change" in dark mode, so the white text must stay.
- **Suggested fix:** remove `dark:text-gm-navy` from these two navy badges. Size S. VISIBLE (dark mode only; it restores what light mode shows).

#### 4. Nothing on the site shows that it has been pressed

- **Where:** the whole app. A search for `active:` in `apps/web/gms/src` finds no pressed style on any link card, tab, menu row, day tab or footer link; only the shared `Button` brings its own.
- **Rule or reference:** `docs/agents/extended-rules.md`: "Because the tap highlight is off, every tappable control needs its own `:active` state." mobile-native, "Tap feels laggy": feedback on press, not on release.
- **Why it matters:** on a phone the browser's grey flash is switched off by the shared layer and hover does not exist, so a tap on a day tab, a menu row or a link card gives no sign it registered until the next page arrives. This is the main reason the site feels like a web page and not an app on a phone.
- **Suggested fix:** one pressed style per family (day tabs and Weather now tabs: a lighter fill; link cards and menu rows: the surface colour; text links: underline), 100 to 150 milliseconds, colour only. Size S to M. VISIBLE (while a finger is down).

#### 5. Form fields and buttons are 32 pixels tall on phones, and two selects make iPhones zoom

- **Where:** every form uses the shared `Input`, `NativeSelect` and default `Button` unchanged: `alert-email-signup.tsx`, `newsletter-signup.tsx`, `data-request-form.tsx`, `src/components/site-search-form.tsx` and the button on `src/app/not-found.tsx`. Measured at 390 pixels: text inputs 358 by 32, "Email me alerts" 127 by 32, "Search" 358 by 32, "Go to the forecast" 146 by 32. The two selects on the data request form ("Intended use", "Format") have 14 pixel text.
- **Rule or reference:** lane spec, Layout: "Touch targets ≥44px on mobile"; Forms: "16px inputs on mobile". mobile-native: iOS zooms the page on any field under 16 pixels and does not zoom back.
- **Suggested fix:** in this app give form fields and submit buttons `h-11` (the lane's header button already uses it) and the selects 16 pixel text on phones. Size S. VISIBLE (taller controls).

#### 6. A form error is one line under the button and no field is marked

- **Where:** `data-request-form.tsx` lines 47 to 61 and 263 to 273 (also the two shorter forms). Only the first problem is reported, as a red sentence after the submit button; no field gets `aria-invalid`, a message of its own, or a link from the message.
- **Rule or reference:** lane spec, Forms: "errors beside the field, focus the first error on submit". fixing-accessibility, forms and errors: errors linked to their fields, invalid fields marked.
- **Why it matters:** the data request has ten fields over four phone screens. After a failed submit the person sees one sentence at the very bottom, fixes that field, submits again and is told about the next one.
- **Suggested fix:** show every problem beside its field, mark the field, and move focus to the first one; keep the summary line for server failures. Size M. VISIBLE (error state only).

#### 7. Tap targets still under 44 pixels after pull request #1732

Measured at 390 pixels. #1732 fixed the location tabs, breadcrumbs and footer; these remain.

- The onward link beside each home section title ("All desk posts", "More stories", "Climate", "All questions", "Open interactive map"): 20 pixels tall. `src/components/home/home-section.tsx` line 47.
- Card links on the home page ("All issued products", "See the whole week", "Read the article", "Open the full satellite page"): 20 pixels tall. `src/components/home/live-sections.tsx`, `data-sections.tsx`, `weather-now-panel.tsx`.
- The six sector icons on the Plan your week card: 40 by 40. `src/components/home/data-sections.tsx` line 204.
- Frequently asked questions: only the question text opens the answer (24 pixels tall on a one-line question); the padding belongs to the card, not the question, so tapping the card's edge does nothing. `src/components/pages/faq-list.tsx` lines 14 to 18. The same pattern is on `/help` and the CAP feed page.
- Search results on `/search`: only the title text is a link (24 pixels), inside a card that looks tappable. `src/app/(pages)/search/page.tsx` lines 72 to 78.
- The masthead logo (87 by 36) and the status pill (40 by 44, two pixels a side short). `src/components/header.tsx` line 109, `src/components/warning-status-pill.tsx` line 19.
- "Share update" (32 tall) and "Open update" (24 tall) on From the Desk. `src/components/product-update-feed.tsx`.
- Sitemap links are 22 pixels tall with 8 pixels between them. `src/app/(pages)/sitemap/page.tsx`.
- **Rule or reference:** lane spec: "≥24px everywhere, ≥44px for primary touch targets".
- **Suggested fix:** `min-h-11` on the onward links and the logo link, `min-w-11` on the pill, padding moved onto the `summary`, the whole search result card as the link, more space between sitemap links. Size S. INVISIBLE for the hit areas; the sitemap spacing and sector icon size are VISIBLE.

#### 8. The "Not yet available" notice borrows the yellow warning colour

- **Where:** `src/components/pages/placeholder-notice.tsx` lines 47 and 56 (`border-gm-risk-yellow`). It appears on every page whose product is not issued yet.
- **Rule or reference:** lane spec, Don'ts: "Use a hazard colour for decoration, or a brand hue to signal danger." Principles: "Colour is meaning."
- **Why it matters:** on a warning service's site, a box outlined in the "Be aware" yellow reads at a glance like a yellow notice. It is the most repeated coloured box on the site.
- **Suggested fix:** use the ordinary border with the surface fill, or the sky-ink accent used for kickers. Size S. VISIBLE.

#### 9. The site's main shortcuts lead to pages that have nothing to show yet

- **Where:** the phone menu's four quick links start with Radar and Tropics (`src/components/nav-drawer.tsx` lines 26 to 31); the search dialog's "Popular" list includes Radar and Marine forecast (`src/components/site-search.tsx` lines 23 to 28); the footer's Weather column lists Radar second (`src/components/footer.tsx` line 19). The Weather menu on desktop has 30 links, 18 of them marked "Coming".
- **Rule or reference:** refactoring-ui, hierarchy: the most prominent place goes to what matters most. The owner's rule keeps these pages and their "Coming" labels, so this finding is only about which links get the shortcut places.
- **Why it matters:** a first-time visitor on a phone who taps the first quick link gets a page that says three times that radar is not available.
- **Suggested fix:** until the feeds exist, give the shortcut places to live pages (Today, Alerts, Satellite, Observations), and list live pages before "Coming" ones inside each menu group. Nothing is hidden. Size S. VISIBLE.

#### 10. Three footer social buttons open the platforms' own home pages

- **Where:** `src/components/footer.tsx` lines 74, 85 and 86: X, YouTube and LinkedIn point to `https://x.com`, `https://youtube.com` and `https://linkedin.com`. The comment marks them as placeholders until GMS supplies its accounts.
- **Rule or reference:** lane spec, Voice: "credible and informed". web-design-guidelines, content: a link goes where its label says.
- **Why it matters:** "GMS on YouTube" that opens YouTube's front page looks broken on an official site, and during an event people use these buttons to find the official account.
- **Suggested fix:** show only the networks with a real account (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp today) and add the others when GMS sends the addresses. Size S. VISIBLE. Needs the owner to confirm with GMS.

#### 11. On a phone the footer is nearly two screens tall on every page

- **Where:** `src/components/footer.tsx`. Measured at 390 pixels: 1,571 pixels. On short pages it is longer than the page itself (frequently asked questions: about 770 pixels of content, then the footer; radar is similar).
- **Rule or reference:** refactoring-ui, hierarchy and spacing. mobile-native: keep the content in reach of a thumb.
- **Why it matters:** the footer repeats the main menu as four open columns of 44 pixel rows. The alert sign-up and the emergency advice, which the owner placed first, are worth their space; the link columns are not.
- **Suggested fix:** on phones fold the four link columns into four closed rows that open on tap (the same pattern as the phone menu), keeping the alerts box, the emergency box, the contacts and the legal row as they are. Size M. VISIBLE.

#### 12. From the Desk cards stretch to the tallest card

- **Where:** the row in `src/components/home/live-sections.tsx` (line 311). On a phone every card is 578 pixels tall, so the pinned "Public Weather Forecast" card shows three lines of text, about 380 pixels of blank space and then its link. On desktop three cards sit in a four-column grid with one column empty.
- **Rule or reference:** refactoring-ui, layout and spacing: do not let a container's size be decided by an unrelated neighbour. Lane spec, From the Desk feed: the next card peeks on phones.
- **Suggested fix:** let cards keep their own height in the swipe row (align to the top), or give the short official card the picture area the other cards have. Size S. VISIBLE.

#### 13. The Weather now tabs cannot be moved with the arrow keys

- **Where:** `src/components/home/weather-now-panel.tsx` lines 123 to 146. The five buttons have `role="tab"` and `aria-selected`, but each is its own tab stop and the arrow keys do nothing.
- **Rule or reference:** fixing-accessibility, semantics: "if a role is used, required aria attributes [and behaviour] must be present". Screen reader users are told "tab, 1 of 5" and expect the arrow keys to work.
- **Suggested fix:** use the shared `Tabs` primitive the lane spec already names, or add arrow-key movement with one tab stop. Size S. INVISIBLE.

#### 14. Three pages have no proper page title

- **Where:** `/explore/updates` has no first-level heading at all; its title "From the Desk" is a second-level heading (`src/components/gms-news.tsx` line 25). The disclaimer and accessibility pages write their own smaller title instead of using `PageHeader` (`src/app/(pages)/disclaimer/page.tsx` line 6, `src/app/(pages)/accessibility/page.tsx` line 6), so they look different from every other standing page.
- **Rule or reference:** lane spec: "one `h1`"; "Use `PageHeader`/`PageSection` … instead of hand-rolled `h1`/`h2`."
- **Suggested fix:** use `PageHeader` on all three. Size S. The heading level is INVISIBLE; the title style is VISIBLE.

### Polish

#### 15. Three classes in the hero name a text size that does not exist

- **Where:** `src/components/home/hero-card.tsx` lines 104, 110 and 114 use `text-body-lg leading-body-lg`. The type scale has no such step, so the classes do nothing; on staging the sky words under the big temperature ("Lightning") and the high and low beside it measured 16 pixels, the inherited size.
- **Rule or reference:** lane spec, Typography: the named steps only. ui-polish-pass, token discipline.
- **Suggested fix:** use `text-body-base leading-body-base` (keeps today's look; INVISIBLE), or `text-nav leading-nav` if the larger size was intended (VISIBLE). Size S.

#### 16. Menu and card links reload the whole page

- **Where:** plain `<a href>` for internal addresses in the phone menu (`src/components/nav-drawer.tsx` lines 106, 184 and 203), the link cards (`src/components/pages/link-list.tsx` line 16, used by every section index), the Discover cards (`src/components/home/discover.tsx` line 182) and news cards (`src/components/news.tsx` line 43).
- **Rule or reference:** lane spec, Accessibility: "real `<a>`/`<Link>` for navigation". With `Link` the next page is fetched ahead and the masthead does not repaint.
- **Suggested fix:** use `Link` where the address is internal. Size S. INVISIBLE (faster page changes).

#### 17. Sentences set in the 11 pixel label size

- **Where:** table captions (`src/components/pages/info-table.tsx` lines 60 and 99, for example "Last observed 19:00 AST" and the CAP feed notes), event dates and places on the events page, and the imagery caption on the radar page. Measured at 11 pixels on phone and desktop.
- **Rule or reference:** lane spec, Typography: 11 pixels is the "Label, table head" step (bold, short, uppercase); captions are 12 pixels and small body text 13.
- **Suggested fix:** use `text-caption` or `text-body-sm` for anything that is a sentence. Size S. VISIBLE.

#### 18. A placeholder page says the same thing three times

- **Where:** `/weather/radar` (`src/app/(pages)/weather/radar/page.tsx` lines 19 to 22) shows the notice "Radar imagery will appear here once GMS publishes it", an empty frame reading "Radar imagery will appear here once a feed is connected", and a caption "Radar loop — imagery source not yet connected". Satellite follows the same pattern when its feed is empty.
- **Rule or reference:** refactoring-ui, hierarchy: one place for each fact. The owner's rule (keep the structure, show the notice) is kept.
- **Suggested fix:** keep the notice and the frame; leave the frame without its own sentence, or drop the caption until there is imagery to caption. Size S. VISIBLE.

#### 19. The phone browser's bar does not match the navy masthead

- **Where:** no `theme-color` is set (`src/lib/site-metadata.ts`, `src/app/layout.tsx`); the script found none on any page.
- **Rule or reference:** mobile-native, "Status bar color doesn't match": set `theme-color` per colour scheme.
- **Suggested fix:** set it to the navy of the masthead for both themes. Size S. VISIBLE (the browser bar on phones).

#### 20. The footer jumped once while the home page loaded on a slow connection

- **Where:** the home page's loading placeholder (`src/app/(weather)/loading.tsx`) is one block and three tiles, far shorter than the page that replaces it. On the first throttled load the footer was pushed down after 2.7 seconds (layout shift score 0.16; 0.1 is the usual limit). A second throttled load of the same page, and of three others, scored zero, so this depends on what arrives first.
- **Rule or reference:** lane spec, States: "loading (skeleton matching final layout, no shift)".
- **Suggested fix:** give the placeholder the height of the hero and the first two sections, or reserve a minimum height for the main area. Size S. INVISIBLE.

#### 21. Small hardening

- **Quiz focus.** After an answer is chosen every option is disabled, so a keyboard user's focus falls back to the top of the page (`src/components/pages/weather-quiz.tsx` line 64). Keep the buttons focusable and mark them `aria-disabled`, or move focus to the explanation. Size S. INVISIBLE.
- **Phone menu.** The menu is a dialog without `aria-modal`, and its scrolling list does not stop the scroll from chaining to the page (the shared rule sits on the dialog, but the list inside is what scrolls; `src/components/nav-drawer.tsx` lines 87 to 93 and 119 to 122). Size S. INVISIBLE.
- **Search dialog on phones** has no visible close button; it closes by tapping outside (`src/components/site-search.tsx`). Add one. Size S. VISIBLE.
- **Last-resort error page** (`src/app/global-error.tsx`) is unstyled browser text. Give it inline styles, the navy title and a 44 pixel button. Size S. VISIBLE (rare).
- **Desktop menu edge.** In the 1280 pixel screenshot the open menu panel starts about five pixels in from the left edge, leaving a sliver of the page showing (`src/components/desktop-nav.tsx` lines 256 to 264). Worth a look on a real screen. Size S. VISIBLE.
- **Footer social row** wraps five and one at desktop width, leaving WhatsApp alone on a second line. Size S. VISIBLE.
- **Email fields** have no `spellCheck={false}`, and the "Elements needed" legend uses raw `text-sm` instead of a named step (`data-request-form.tsx` line 172). Size S. INVISIBLE.

## What could not be checked

- **A real phone.** Pressed feedback, tap delay, the zoom on the two selects, the browser bar colour and the swipe row were argued from the stylesheets and tested only in an emulated phone viewport. mobile-native's rule is that these need a real device.
- **Submitting a form.** Nothing was submitted, so the error wording, the success messages, the human check and finding 2 (the form before the page is ready) were read from code only. The human check did not render on staging.
- **Warnings in effect.** Staging had no active warning, so the warning ribbon, the red takeover on the home page, the alert detail page and the yellow, amber and red states of the status pill were audited from code only. Only the "No active alerts" and "Ended" states were seen.
- **A live forecast and fresh data.** Staging had no issued forecast and a 21-day-old reading, so the day tabs, day panels and the fresh "Now" state were seen only in their empty or stale forms.
- **Real editorial content.** From the Desk, Weather news, Questions and the article pages held test entries or none, so long headlines, photo credits and the media players were read from code only (break-ui was argued, not run).
- **Screen readers.** Reasoned from the markup and the axe results; no screen reader was run.
- **Dark mode on every page.** Eight pages were captured in dark mode by setting the theme class, and two more through the real theme button; the rest were not viewed in dark.
- **Production.** Only staging was visited.
- **The installed app view.** The bottom tab bar is reserved for the future GMS app and is not shown on the website, so it was read from code only.
- **Carriacou.** The location page loaded, but without station data it shows the national forecast only; nothing more could be judged.
