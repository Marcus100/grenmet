# Design audit: Grenada Signal (2026-11)

**Status:** Phase 1 audit, read only. Nothing was changed in the app; every fix below is a proposal for the owner.  
**Owner:** Barrels Grenada engineering (design mastery programme, issue #1453, section 8)  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-23

## Scope and method

Surfaces audited, all in `apps/web/signal`:

- the front page (`/`): masthead, the green briefing panel, "The stories to know", "The Latest", the collection, the topic blocks and "Keep reading";
- reading: a story page (`/<section>/<slug>`) and a Daily Signal edition (`/today/<date>`);
- finding things: topic pages, `/topics`, `/briefs`, `/learn`, `/archive`, `/collections/<slug>`, `/search`;
- the newsletter: the sign-up form on `/about#newsletter` and the result page `/subscribe/status`;
- the not-found, error and loading pages, the privacy page, the header and the footer.

The rule for judging: a defect is a gap against this app's own lane spec ([`docs/design/signal.md`](../signal.md)), its `AGENTS.md`, or a rule from a design skill, never a difference from another app. Signal is a reading product, so typography was judged first: line length, text size, line height, how headlines break, and how all of it reads on a phone.

Method:

1. Read the instructions (`AGENTS.md` at the root and in `apps/web/signal`), the lane spec, and the section "Reviewing or polishing UI: check these before reporting a gap" in `docs/agents/extended-rules.md`. The code was read from `origin/dev` at commit `e7d2b4f6b`.
2. Checked where styles live before reporting anything. Signal's stylesheet (`src/app/globals.css`) imports the shared layer `packages/ui/src/styles/app-feel.css`, which already switches off the grey tap flash, sets `touch-action: manipulation` on links and controls, and cancels animation for people who ask for less motion. `globals.css` itself draws a green keyboard focus ring on every link, button and input (gold on the green panel) and lets long words wrap. Tailwind's `hover:` styles apply only where the device can hover, so there are no stuck hover states on phones. Nothing in this audit repeats a gap those files already cover.
3. Read every page and shared component, then applied the rules of these skills: ui-polish-pass (reading defaults), web-design-guidelines (pinned rules), fixing-accessibility, mobile-native, emil-design-eng, break-ui (worst-case content, argued from the code), refactoring-ui (hierarchy first), review-animations and find-animation-opportunities. The app has no motion at all, so the motion review produced only the small suggestions in finding 17.
4. Looked at the running staging app (`signal.staging.barrels.gd`) with a scripted browser at 390 pixels with touch (phone), 320 pixels and 1280 pixels (desktop), in light and dark settings: the front page, two stories, the 3 October edition, the editions list, a topic, all topics, the archive, search (one word typed; search runs on the device), About with the newsletter form (looked at, never submitted), the newsletter result page, a missing page and the privacy page. Text size, line height, characters per line, page height and the size of every link were measured in the page. Dark mode was loaded only to confirm the "light mode" rule holds (it does: the page stays white with dark ink). The screenshots were viewed during the audit and are not committed.
5. Collected reference screens from Mobbin for the main journeys (see "Inspiration"), for ideas only.

Not re-reported here, because they are already in GitHub issue 1688: the seven-week-old briefing date on the front page, the height of the front page on a phone, the old page-title typo, the missing "No matches" line in search, and the general count of small tap targets. Two owner rules were kept in mind throughout: empty sections stay visible, and the site stays indexed.

How to read a finding:

- **Rule or reference** is the lane spec line, skill rule or reference behaviour behind it.
- **Size:** S is under half a day, M is about a day, L is several days or needs a decision first.
- **INVISIBLE** means readers would not see a difference in normal use (accessibility, focus, keyboard, share previews, behaviour on failure). These can merge on green checks. **VISIBLE** means layout, colour or wording changes, so the owner reviews before and after screenshots first.

## What is already strong

- **The reading column is right.** Story and edition text is 18 pixels with a 32 pixel line height in a 640 pixel column: 73 characters per line on a desktop and about 39 on a phone, both inside the comfortable range. Summaries and introductions are 18 pixels too, and no text on the site is under 12 pixels.
- **The page never scrolls sideways.** At 320, 390 and 1280 pixels the page width equals the screen width, and long words wrap (`overflow-wrap: anywhere` in `globals.css`).
- **Structure is honest.** One first-level heading per page, a skip link that is the first Tab stop, a named main navigation and footer navigation, a language on the page, and a visible green focus ring with a gold version on the green panel.
- **Photographs never jump the page.** `StoryImage` reserves the picture's shape before it loads, every picture has descriptive alternative text and a linked credit, and a story without a picture shows no empty frame.
- **Empty states are written, not hidden.** A topic with no stories, an empty guides page, an empty collection and an empty search each say what is happening and offer a next step, which matches the owner rule.
- **The newsletter is careful with consent.** The email field is labelled, 16 pixels (phones do not zoom), with the right keyboard and autofill; consent is a separate tick box; the address is confirmed by a second email; the result page is kept out of search engines.
- **The voice is consistent.** Proper apostrophes and quotation marks, a real ellipsis in "Sending…", and warm short headings ("Worth another read.", "Less jargon. More understanding.").
- **Preview material is labelled where it matters.** Every story and edition carries its "Editorial preview" or "Sample story" note above the text, and sources are listed at the end.

## Findings

### Blocking

#### 1. The newsletter form can put a reader's email address in the page address

- **Where:** `src/components/subscribe-form.tsx` lines 86 to 124. The form has only an `onSubmit` handler, no `method`, and the "Subscribe" button is live as soon as the page is drawn. On staging the form's method is the browser default (`get`) and the button is not disabled.
- **What happens:** on a slow phone connection the form is on screen before its script has attached. A reader who types an address and presses "Subscribe" in that gap gets a plain browser submit: the page reloads as `/about?email=<their address>&consent=on`, nothing is subscribed, and nothing tells them so. The address then sits in browser history, in server and proxy logs, and in the page address that page analytics records.
- **Rule or reference:** `docs/agents/extended-rules.md`, Form patterns: "A page-level form that only has `onSubmit` falls back to a native GET before hydration, which puts passwords and personal data in the URL. Disable its submit until hydrated." The auth and bookings apps already do this with `useHydrated()`.
- **Why it matters:** this is the one place Signal asks for personal data, and the privacy page promises it is handled with care. Checked from the code and the rendered form only; nothing was submitted.
- **Suggested fix:** keep the button disabled until the form's script is ready (the `useHydrated()` pattern), and set `method="post"` on the form as a second guard. Size S. INVISIBLE.

### Should fix

#### 2. On a phone, the masthead fills almost half the screen on every page, including stories

- **Where:** `src/components/site-header.tsx` lines 19 to 64. At 390 pixels the place-name strip, the wordmark, the tagline and ten navigation links wrapped over three rows take 379 pixels of an 844 pixel screen (419 when "Sign in" shows). On a story the headline starts at about 500 pixels and the first paragraph of the text at about 1,290 pixels, a screen and a half down.
- **Rule or reference:** the lane spec asks for a "substantial centred wordmark" and "wrapping navigation", so the front page follows the spec. The gap is on inner pages: refactoring-ui, hierarchy (the thing the reader came for should lead), and every news reference in "Inspiration" (a slim bar with the name once the reader is inside a story).
- **Why it matters:** most readers will arrive on a story from a shared link, on a phone. What they see first is the publication's name, tagline and menu, not the story.
- **Suggested fix:** keep the full masthead on the front page; on stories and editions at phone width show a compact header (wordmark at a smaller size, no tagline, the navigation as one row that scrolls sideways or behind a "Sections" link). This changes a recorded spec line, so it needs the owner's yes. Size M. VISIBLE.

#### 3. Headlines and paragraphs break wherever they fall

- **Where:** no file in the app or in the shared styles sets `text-wrap`. Measured on staging, every headline and paragraph reports `text-wrap: wrap`. The story headline on a phone ends with one word alone on its third line ("next?"), the front page heading ends with "Signal" alone, and several summaries end on a single word.
- **Rule or reference:** ui-polish-pass, reading defaults: "`text-wrap: pretty` on body text so paragraphs do not end on a lone word"; web-design-guidelines, Typography (balance headings).
- **Why it matters:** in a publication the headline is the design. Even lines and no stranded last word are the cheapest way to make every page look edited.
- **Suggested fix:** in `src/app/globals.css`, set `text-wrap: balance` on headings and `text-wrap: pretty` on paragraphs, list items and captions. Browsers that do not support it ignore it. Size S. VISIBLE (line breaks shift slightly).

#### 4. The section name is printed twice above every story headline, and the first one is a 20 pixel tap target

- **Where:** `src/app/[section]/[slug]/page.tsx` lines 66 to 74. A grey "← NEWS & COMMUNITY" link sits directly above a green "NEWS & COMMUNITY" label with a gold rule. The link is 20 pixels tall; the source links at the end of the story (lines 97 to 110) are 17 pixels tall on a desktop.
- **Rule or reference:** refactoring-ui, hierarchy: one place for each fact. Lane spec, Accessibility: "Main actions are at least 44px tall."
- **Why it matters:** two lines saying the same thing push the headline down and make the top of the story look unfinished; the way back to the topic is hard to hit with a thumb.
- **Suggested fix:** keep one: make the green label itself the link to the topic, with a 44 pixel hit area, and give each source link a taller row. Size S. VISIBLE.

#### 5. A story ends in a dead end

- **Where:** `src/app/[section]/[slug]/page.tsx` line 115. After the sources box the only thing offered is the general "Stay curious. Keep reading." block, the same on every page of the site. There is no next story from the same topic, no way to share, and no route to the newsletter.
- **Rule or reference:** lane spec, Layout: "Discovery and archive links provide the next reading action." The reference screens (Quartz, Tripadvisor, the New York Times) all end a story with two or three related stories.
- **Why it matters:** the owner wants traffic and repeat visits. A reader who finishes a story is the most likely person to read another, and the page offers a tour of the site instead of a story.
- **Suggested fix:** above "Keep reading", add "More in <topic>": up to three headlines from the same topic (the homepage card rules still hold: no source or date lines). A share row (copy link, WhatsApp, and the phone's own share sheet) is a separate, larger step that needs an owner decision. Size M for related stories, M for sharing. VISIBLE.

#### 6. Shared links have no picture, and the phone's browser bar has no brand colour

- **Where:** story metadata in `src/app/[section]/[slug]/page.tsx` lines 32 to 50 sets a title, description and date for share previews but no image; `src/app/layout.tsx` lines 37 to 52 sets the small "summary" card and no theme colour. On staging every page reports no `og:image` and no `theme-color`.
- **Rule or reference:** mobile-native, "Status bar color doesn't match: `theme-color`". For share previews, the reference is how every news link looks in WhatsApp and Facebook: a large picture with the headline.
- **Why it matters:** in Grenada news travels through WhatsApp and Facebook. A Signal link currently shows as bare text beside other publishers' picture cards. Most stories already have a licensed photograph in `src/lib/story-images.ts`.
- **Suggested fix:** pass the story's photograph as the share image and switch to the large card; add a default share image with the wordmark for pages without a photograph; add a white `themeColor` in the root layout. Size S to M. INVISIBLE on the site itself.

#### 7. On the front page, the second column's title is louder than the section's title, and the date appears twice

- **Where:** `src/components/lead-stories.tsx`. "The stories to know" (line 74) is a 26 pixel serif heading; "The Latest" (line 103), which sits inside that section, is a 30 pixel bold sans heading at the same heading level. The date "Saturday, 3 October 2026" is shown in the green panel and again, eight lines lower, at the right of "The stories to know" (lines 81 to 86).
- **Rule or reference:** refactoring-ui, hierarchy: "De-emphasise the competition instead of amplifying the target." The lane spec's type list has one section title style (serif, 24 pixels, bold).
- **Why it matters:** the first screen of the front page has four competing headings in three styles. The eye cannot tell that "The Latest" is part of "The stories to know".
- **Suggested fix:** make "The Latest" a third-level heading no larger than the section title (the spec's section-title style), and show the date once. Size S. VISIBLE.

#### 8. Nothing responds when a link is pressed, and the menu does not show where you are

- **Where:** there is no `active:` style anywhere in `apps/web/signal/src`, and the shared layer has switched off the browser's own tap flash. In the main navigation (`site-header.tsx` lines 49 to 63) no link is marked as the current page; on staging, zero navigation links carry `aria-current` on a topic page.
- **Rule or reference:** `docs/agents/extended-rules.md`: "Because the tap highlight is off, every tappable control needs its own `:active` state." Web-design-guidelines, Navigation and State (show the current location).
- **Why it matters:** on a phone a tap on a headline or a menu item gives no sign it was received until the next page arrives, which on a slow connection feels broken. A reader on "Sport" cannot see that they are in Sport.
- **Suggested fix:** a pressed style for headline links, menu links and the green text links (the darker green, or a slight dimming), and `aria-current="page"` with an underline in the gold rule colour on the current menu item. Size S. VISIBLE (the pressed state shows only during a press).

#### 9. The newsletter form reports errors far from the field and loses the reader's place

- **Where:** `src/components/subscribe-form.tsx`. The single error line is drawn under the button (lines 125 to 129), is not tied to the email field or the tick box (`aria-describedby` and `aria-invalid` are never set), and focus stays on the button. On success (lines 76 to 83) the whole form is replaced by one sentence, the button the reader had focus on disappears, and the sentence does not repeat the address. On the result page (`src/app/subscribe/status/page.tsx` lines 10 to 23 and 43 to 45) "signing up again" is plain text with no link to the form, and "Back to Signal" is 20 pixels tall.
- **Rule or reference:** fixing-accessibility, forms and errors: "errors must be linked to fields using aria-describedby", "invalid fields must set aria-invalid". Web-design-guidelines, Forms: errors next to the field, focus the first error.
- **Why it matters:** a screen reader user hears "Please enter a valid email." with no field named; a sighted reader on a phone may not see a red line that appears below the button. After success, a reader who mistyped has no way to see what they sent.
- **Suggested fix:** show each error under its own field and tie it to the field, move focus to the first field in error, move focus to the success message and include the address in it ("We sent a link to …"), link "signing up again" to `/about#newsletter`, and give "Back to Signal" a 44 pixel hit area. Size S. INVISIBLE for the wiring; the error position is VISIBLE.

#### 10. The newsletter has no pitch and is hard to find

- **Where:** `src/app/about/page.tsx` lines 49 to 57. The only form is the last section of the About page, reached from one footer link. The text says "Sign up for email updates" and explains the confirmation step, but not what arrives or how often. The edition page (`src/app/today/[date]/page.tsx`) ends without mentioning that the briefing can come by email.
- **Rule or reference:** the app's `AGENTS.md` records the owner's rule: one form on `/about#newsletter`, "No subscribe button in the masthead or on story pages". This finding respects that for stories and the masthead. The references (Quartz "Daily Brief", the New York Times newsletter page) lead with one sentence of promise and the frequency.
- **Why it matters:** the Daily Signal is the product a reader would subscribe to, and the moment they finish an edition is the moment to ask. Today that reader has to find the footer.
- **Suggested fix:** rewrite the pitch with the promise and the frequency once the owner sets them ("The Daily Signal in your inbox each morning"), and ask the owner whether the end of an edition and the editions list may carry a one-line link to the form (a link, not a second form). Size S for the wording, S for the links, after an owner decision. VISIBLE.

#### 11. The photo caption on the lead story is below the contrast limit

- **Where:** `src/components/lead-stories.tsx` lines 24 to 37 place `StoryImage` inside the tinted lead card (`bg-secondary`, `#f3f4f6`); the caption (`src/components/story-image.tsx` line 37) is 12 pixel grey (`#6b7280`). The ratio is 4.39 to 1; small text needs 4.5 to 1. On white the same grey is 4.83 to 1 and passes.
- **Rule or reference:** lane spec, Typography and colour: "Muted text is used only on white backgrounds."
- **Why it matters:** this is the smallest text on the site, on the most-seen card. The 12 pixel size is the owner's choice and stays.
- **Suggested fix:** on the tinted card only, use the ink colour at reduced strength or a darker grey that passes. This needs either an existing token or a named new one. Size S. INVISIBLE (a slightly darker grey).

#### 12. An edition ends in the wrong order and has no way to the previous one

- **Where:** `src/app/today/[date]/page.tsx` lines 58 to 75. After the briefing comes the full-width "Keep reading" block, and only then a narrow "Read more" list of four stories, which reads as a leftover under the page's closing section. The four stories are simply the first four of all published stories (line 36), not the ones the edition talks about. There is no link to the previous or next edition.
- **Rule or reference:** refactoring-ui, layout: related things sit together, and the closing block closes the page. The Co–Star and New York Times references both let a reader step between days.
- **Why it matters:** the Daily Signal is the habit-forming product. A reader who has finished today's should be one tap from yesterday's.
- **Suggested fix:** put "Read more" directly under the briefing and before "Keep reading", choose stories the edition does not already link to, and add "Previous edition" and "Next edition" links. Size S to M. VISIBLE.

### Nice to have

#### 13. The archive is one very long wall of photo cards, and it promises dates it does not show

- **Where:** `src/app/archive/page.tsx` lines 15 to 18 and 37 to 41. The introduction says stories are shown "with their original publication dates", but the cards (by the owner's rule for discovery cards) carry no date. All 26 stories are drawn as full photo cards: 13,752 pixels tall on a phone, about sixteen screens.
- **Rule or reference:** web-design-guidelines, Content and Copy (the page must do what its text says); refactoring-ui, hierarchy (an archive is a list to scan, not a front page).
- **Suggested fix:** group the archive under month headings ("October 2026", "June 2026"), which gives dates without adding a date line to any card, and use the compact headline row (no photograph) here. Size M. VISIBLE.

#### 14. Lines are too long on the text pages at desktop width

- **Where:** `PageIntro` (`src/components/editorial.tsx` line 19, `max-w-3xl`) and the text of About and Privacy (`max-w-3xl`). Measured at 1280 pixels: 80 characters per line on About and Privacy, 90 in the archive introduction. Stories are 73.
- **Rule or reference:** lane spec, Layout: "Article and edition bodies retain a comfortable `max-w-2xl` measure." The usual comfortable range is 45 to 75 characters.
- **Suggested fix:** hold introductions and the About and Privacy text to the same `max-w-2xl` measure as stories. Size S. VISIBLE.

#### 15. Wording that reads wrongly

- The topics page prints "1 sample stories" for a topic with one story, and calls the October editorial previews "sample stories" (`src/app/topics/page.tsx` line 47). Say "1 story" and "3 stories".
- Search results label every result "· Demo" (`src/components/search-reader.tsx` line 46), including editorial previews, which the rest of the site no longer calls demo.
- The missing-page tab title falls back to "Grenada Signal — Know what is going on." while the front page uses "Grenada Signal: know what is going on" (`src/app/layout.tsx` line 41). Give the not-found page its own title and use one form of the line.
- The site description in `layout.tsx` line 45 still says "Grenada news, weather, and verification in 5 minutes", the earlier positioning; the masthead now says "News, culture and what's worth your time".
- The privacy page tells readers to use "the contact page on the Barrels Grenada website at barrels.gd" without a link (`src/app/privacy/page.tsx` lines 58 to 62).
- Arrows are typed inside link text ("Explore the archive →", "News & Community →"), so a screen reader reads "right arrow" each time. Wrap the arrow so it is not read.
- Rule: web-design-guidelines, Content and Copy; break-ui (a count of exactly one). Size S in total. VISIBLE, except the arrow change, which is INVISIBLE.

#### 16. Small type differences from the lane spec

- Story titles on cards use a 1.25 line height (`leading-tight`, `editorial.tsx` lines 51 to 56); the spec's story title is 1.375. Two-line 24 pixel serif titles look tight on a phone.
- The lead headline is bold in "The stories to know" (`lead-stories.tsx`) and semibold in `StoryCard`; the story page headline is bold and every other page heading is semibold. The spec says bold for lead headlines.
- Story text is dark grey (the typography plugin's `prose-neutral`), while summaries and every other page use the ink colour. The spec says "ink on white for reading". Both pass contrast; the difference is consistency.
- Size S. VISIBLE (subtle).

#### 17. A little motion would help, and only a little

- **Now:** there is no transition anywhere. Hover colour changes snap, and the newsletter form is swapped for the success sentence in one frame.
- **Rule or reference:** find-animation-opportunities and emil-design-eng: motion only where it explains a change or confirms an action; never on reading text.
- **Suggested, all optional:** a 120 millisecond colour change on link hover; the pressed state from finding 8; a 150 millisecond fade when the form becomes the success message. Nothing on scroll, no moving headlines, no animated photographs. The shared layer already cancels all of it for readers who ask for less motion. Size S. VISIBLE.

#### 18. Loading and pictures while they arrive

- The loading skeleton (`src/app/loading.tsx` line 13) uses a 1,152 pixel container with 24 pixel gutters; the pages use 1,280 with 32, and stories 672. The grey shapes therefore sit in a different place from the content that replaces them. Match the page it stands in for.
- A photograph's frame is blank white until the picture arrives (`story-image.tsx` lines 22 to 27). A light tint from the existing secondary colour in the frame would show that a picture is coming without being a placeholder box for stories that have none.
- Cards in the three-column grids are about 380 pixels wide, but every picture asks the browser for a 640 pixel file (`sizes`, `story-image.tsx` line 33). Give the grid cards their real size.
- The root layout loads Noto Sans as well as Inter (`layout.tsx` lines 16 to 26), but `globals.css` sets Signal's text to Inter, so the Noto file appears to be downloaded for nothing. Confirm and remove it from this app.
- Size S each. INVISIBLE.

#### 19. Links inside a briefing reload the whole site

- **Where:** `src/components/mdx-content.tsx` lines 4 to 9 draws every link in story and edition text as a plain link. The edition's "What the notice says →" links point to Signal's own stories, so each tap reloads the page, fonts and all, instead of moving inside the app. The source links at the end of a story open a new tab without saying so.
- **Suggested fix:** use the app's own link component for addresses that start with `/`, and add "(opens in a new tab)" for screen readers on outside links that do. Size S. INVISIBLE.

#### 20. Dates will need more once news is live

- **Now:** a story shows one date ("Saturday, 3 October 2026"), with no time and no "Updated" line; `formatRelativeTime` in `src/lib/format.ts` (line 2) is written but used nowhere. The editorial-preview note hard-codes "3 October 2026" (`editorial.tsx` lines 175 to 181).
- **Why it can wait:** the current content is dated by day only, and the lane spec records "Dates are absolute and rendered in UTC for consistency". When the publishing system brings timed stories, a story published at 9 pm in Grenada would show the next day's date under that rule.
- **Suggested fix, when live news starts:** show Grenada time, add "Updated" when a story changes, and use the relative form ("2 hours ago") for the first day only. Size M, with the owner. VISIBLE.

## Documents that no longer match the app

Small drifts noticed while auditing. Nothing was edited.

- `docs/design/signal.md`, Overview, says the previews are "excluded from indexing"; the same file's Accessibility section and `apps/web/signal/AGENTS.md` say the site is indexed by the owner's decision of 16 November 2026, and staging confirms only the newsletter result page is `noindex`.
- The lane spec calls "More from Signal" a heading; in `lead-stories.tsx` it is a paragraph inside a named navigation.
- The lane spec's story title line height (1.375) and lead headline weight (700) differ from the code (finding 16).
