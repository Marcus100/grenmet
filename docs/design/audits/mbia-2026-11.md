# Design audit: airport passenger site (2026-11)

**Status:** Phase 1 audit, read only. Nothing was changed in the app; every fix below is a proposal for the owner.  
**Owner:** Barrels Grenada engineering (design mastery programme, issue #1453, section 8)  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-23

## Scope and method

Surfaces audited, all in `apps/web/mbia` (the public site for the Grenada Airports Authority: Maurice Bishop International Airport and Lauriston Airport, Carriacou):

- the home page with its short flight board;
- the arrivals and departures board (`/flights`);
- airport information: the Travel Guide section and its pages (parking, ground transportation, passenger information), the At the Airport section (terminal map), and the three airport pages;
- the contact page and its form;
- the header, phone menu, footer, the not-found page, the error page and the loading placeholder.

The rule for judging: a defect is a gap against this app's own lane spec ([`docs/design/mbia.md`](../mbia.md)) or a rule from a design skill, never a difference from another app. The airport's marks and look belong to the client and stay as they are.

Two journeys were judged above all: someone with a phone in the car park who wants to know in seconds whether a flight is on time; and a first-time visitor looking for parking, transport and contact details.

Method:

1. Read the instructions (`AGENTS.md` at the root and in `apps/web/mbia`), the lane spec, section 1 of `docs/exec-plans/gaa-next-wave.md` (what is already planned for this site), and the section "Reviewing or polishing UI: check these before reporting a gap" in `docs/agents/extended-rules.md`.
2. Checked where styles live before reporting anything. The shared layer `packages/ui/src/styles/app-feel.css` is imported by `src/app/globals.css`; it already switches off the tap flash, sets `touch-action: manipulation` on controls, stops long presses selecting button labels, and cancels transitions and animation for people who ask for less motion. Tailwind's `hover:` styles in this app apply only to devices with a real pointer, so hover does not stick on phones. The shared `Button` already has a pressed state. Nothing in this audit repeats a gap those files cover.
3. Read every page, shared component and the content files for the journeys above, then applied the rules of these skills: ui-polish-pass, web-design-guidelines, fixing-accessibility, mobile-native, emil-design-eng, break-ui (worst-case data, argued from the code), review-animations, find-animation-opportunities and the repository's refactoring-ui. Motion in this app is hover lift, a slow image zoom and colour changes only, so the two motion skills found little.
4. Looked at the running staging app (`mbia.staging.barrels.gd`) with a scripted browser at 390 pixels with touch (phone) and 1280 pixels (desktop): home, the board (both tabs and the Lauriston deep link), parking, ground transportation, the Travel Guide and the other five section lists, contact, the airport pages, passenger information, terminal map, the phone menu, the desktop menu, keyboard focus order on the home page and an unknown address. Dark mode was loaded too, only to confirm the lane spec's "light mode only" rule holds (it does: the page stays light). The contact form was not submitted. The screenshots were viewed during the audit and are not committed.
5. Collected reference screens for ideas only (see Inspiration).

Not repeated here: items already recorded in issue #1688 (the sample flight data needing a prominent label or a live source, the phone page length and making the board a compact card list, search by flight number, the Call button in the phone menu) and the tap-target work merged in pull requests #1694 and #1732.

How to read a finding:

- **Rule or reference** is the lane spec line, skill rule or reference behaviour behind it.
- **Size:** S is under half a day, M is about a day, L is several days or needs a decision first.
- **INVISIBLE** means visitors would not see a difference in normal use (accessibility, focus, keyboard, hidden hit areas, behaviour on failure). These can merge on green checks. **VISIBLE** means layout, colour or wording changes, so the owner reviews before and after screenshots first.

## What is already strong

- **Status is always a word.** Every row carries "On time", "Delayed", "Landed", "Cancelled" and so on; colour only supports it. On a phone the status sits under the flight number, so it is never cut off, and the table did not scroll sideways at 390 pixels.
- **The sample board is labelled where it matters.** A gold "Sample schedule, not live flights." line sits above the board on the home page and the board page, and the board page heading repeats it. The code never mixes sample and live rows.
- **The look is consistent and the client's own.** Navy heroes with the gold runway rule, Manrope headings, one gold button per hero, real photographs with descriptive alternative text, tabular digits on the board.
- **The basics of structure are right.** A skip link, one first-level heading per page, a `main` landmark, a language, a real table with column headers, a phone menu that traps focus and returns it to the menu button, a desktop menu built from native disclosure elements that closes on Escape.
- **Keyboard focus is visible** on every control tried on the home page (ring or outline).
- **Failure has a page.** The error page says what happened in plain words, offers "Try again" (using `retry`, which fetches again) and keeps the site navigation. If the flight service is down the labelled sample board shows instead of an error.
- **Form outlines meet contrast** (the planned input-contrast fix from the next-wave plan is in), text fields are 16 pixels so phones do not zoom, and every field has a visible label.
- **No horizontal scroll** at 390 pixels on every page tried except one (finding 1).
- **Public and indexed.** No page carries a no-index tag except the not-found page, which is correct.

## Findings

### Blocking

#### 1. The Travel Guide list is wider than a phone screen, and two cards show raw web addresses

- **Where:** `/travel` (`src/app/[section]/page.tsx` lines 57 to 69, `src/components/page-card.tsx` lines 18 and 29) with the summary text in `content/travel/airlines-serving.mdx` line 3 and `content/travel/passenger-information.mdx` line 3.
- **What happens:** on staging at 390 pixels the page is 507 pixels wide, so every card runs off the right edge and the whole page slides sideways. The cause is the summary of "Airlines Serving Grenada": `American Airlines(https://www.aa.com%20) JetBlue Airways(https://www.jetblue.com%20) …`, long strings with no place to break. The grid column grows to fit them and takes every card with it. The other five section lists are fine.
- **Rule or reference:** break-ui (unbreakable strings); mobile-native baseline (no sideways page scroll); web-design-guidelines, Content: text containers handle long content.
- **Why it matters:** the Travel Guide is the way to parking, transport, check-in and baggage: the second journey starts on a broken page.
- **Suggested fix:** let the card shrink and wrap (`min-w-0` on the card and `overflow-wrap: anywhere` on the summary), and rewrite the two summaries as sentences ("The airlines that fly to Grenada, with links to each."). Fix the converter (`scripts/convert-scrape.py`) so a link never becomes "Name(address)" in a summary. Size S. The wrap guard is INVISIBLE; the new summaries are VISIBLE.

#### 2. Phone numbers and the email address on the contact page cannot be tapped, and nothing says which number is for what

- **Where:** `src/app/contact/page.tsx` lines 12 to 37 and 58 to 65. Staging: no `tel:` or `mailto:` link inside the page body (the footer has one of each).
- **What happens:** three phone numbers, a fax number and the email are plain grey text. There are no opening hours, no label for each number (switchboard, flight enquiries, lost property), no "Get directions" link and no map.
- **Rule or reference:** web-design-guidelines (links are links); mobile-native (phone numbers are tap-to-call). Urban Outfitters' contact page gives each channel its own block with its hours.
- **Why it matters:** a traveller on a phone has to copy a number by hand. This page is the end of the "find contact details" journey.
- **Suggested fix:** make each number a `tel:` link and the email a `mailto:` link at 44 pixels tall, add a "Get directions" link to a maps address for Point Salines, and ask the Authority for the purpose and hours of each number. Keep one source for the numbers (they are typed separately in the header, footer, phone menu and this page). Size S for links and directions (INVISIBLE in look, the text only becomes tappable); the labels and hours are VISIBLE and need the client's facts.

### Should fix

#### 3. On a phone the first flight is a full screen down, and the header has no shortcut to the board

- **Where:** `src/app/page.tsx` line 111 (`min-h-[70vh] pt-32 pb-40`) and line 401; `src/components/site-header.tsx` line 121 (`hidden … sm:inline-flex` on the gold "Flight Info" button).
- **What happens:** at 390 by 844 pixels the board starts at 578 pixels and its first row at 793 pixels, the very bottom of the first screen; one flight is half visible. Below 640 pixels the header shows only the logo and the menu button, so reaching the board from any other page takes two taps (menu, then Flight Information).
- **Rule or reference:** refactoring-ui, hierarchy: the thing most people came for leads. American Airlines' home screen puts "Flight status" first in a row of three round shortcuts.
- **Why it matters:** this is the "in seconds, in a car park" journey. (Issue #1688 covers the page's total length and a card layout; this is about where the board starts and how to reach it.)
- **Suggested fix:** on phones shorten the hero (drop the minimum height and the large bottom padding) so two or three rows show on the first screen, and keep a compact "Flights" button in the phone header. Size S to M. VISIBLE.

#### 4. The short board on the home page has no way to see the rest

- **Where:** `src/components/flight-board.tsx` line 150 (`rows.slice(0, 5)` when `compact`). The home board shows five of six arrivals and stops, with no count and no link.
- **Rule or reference:** web-design-guidelines, Content: a cut list says so and links onward. Lane spec, Agent prompt guide 2 (arrivals widget on home).
- **Why it matters:** a person whose flight is the sixth row sees nothing and may conclude it is not listed. The only route to the full board is the hero button above.
- **Suggested fix:** a footer row inside the board, "All arrivals" or "All departures", linking to `/flights?board=…`. Size S. VISIBLE.

#### 5. The chosen airport is shown by colour alone, and the Arrivals and Departures tabs are tabs in name only

- **Where:** `flight-board.tsx` lines 34 to 60 (tabs) and 70 to 89 (airport chips). Staging: the airport buttons have no `aria-pressed` or `aria-current`; the tabs have `role="tab"` and `aria-selected` but no tab panel, no `aria-controls`, and arrow keys do nothing.
- **Rule or reference:** lane spec, Accessibility: "the arrivals/departures toggle is a real radio group or tabs". Fixing-accessibility: if a role is used, its required attributes and keyboard behaviour must be present; state must not rely on colour alone.
- **Why it matters:** a screen reader says "MBIA GND, button; Lauriston CRU, button" with no hint which board is showing, and announces tabs that do not behave like tabs.
- **Suggested fix:** make both controls radio groups (or toggle buttons with `aria-pressed`), or complete the tab pattern with the table as the panel. Size S. INVISIBLE.

#### 6. Choosing a tab or an airport does not change the address

- **Where:** `src/components/flights-page-board.tsx` reads `?board=` and `?airport=` once; `flight-board.tsx` keeps the choice in component state only. On staging, after tapping Departures the address stayed `/flights`.
- **Rule or reference:** web-design-guidelines, Navigation and State: the address reflects state (tabs, filters) so it survives refresh, Back and sharing.
- **Why it matters:** someone sends "the departures board" to a relative and the link opens arrivals; a refresh in the car park resets the choice.
- **Suggested fix:** write the choice to the address with a shallow replace (`history.replaceState`), keeping the page static. Size S. INVISIBLE.

#### 7. The board has no date and no time zone, and "Delayed" has no new time

- **Where:** `flight-board.tsx` lines 160 to 185 (the header row is "Time, Flight, From"); `src/lib/flights.ts` lines 36 to 50 (a flight has one time, `scheduled`).
- **What happens:** nothing on the board says which day it is for or that times are Grenada time (many readers are abroad, checking for someone arriving). A delayed row shows the scheduled time and the word "Delayed" only. The sample banner has no date either; the live banner does have "Updated … (Grenada time)".
- **Rule or reference:** lane spec, States: "Updated 14:05" and feed-down messages. Singapore Airlines shows "Scheduled 13:55" above a larger "Estimated 14:10" and "Last updated 12:48, 8 Feb 2024"; Google Maps' station board strikes through the old time and prints the new one beside "Delayed 3 min".
- **Why it matters:** "is it on time, and if not, when" is the whole question. This must be designed before a licensed live feed arrives, not after.
- **Suggested fix:** add the board's date and "Grenada time" to the board header now; add an optional estimated time to the flight model, shown as old time struck through with the new time beside the status word; show the "Updated" time on every source. Size M. VISIBLE.

#### 8. When the live source is switched on, each row will read oddly

- **Where:** `src/lib/live-flights.ts` lines 35 to 50 and `flight-board.tsx` lines 213 to 220. The live mapping sets both `city` and `cityCode` to the same four-letter airport code, so a row prints it twice ("KMIA KMIA"); the airline is empty, so the Airline column is blank; the Time cell carries a date and a time ("22 Nov 14:05") in the large display size; the flight is a radio callsign ("AAL1509"). Not seen on staging, where the switch is off.
- **Rule or reference:** break-ui (missing fields and duplicate fields); ui-polish-pass (walk every state, not only the demo data).
- **Why it matters:** the first day the flag is turned on, the public board looks broken.
- **Suggested fix:** print the code once, hide the Airline column when no row has an airline, split date and time, and add a small table of city names for the common origin codes. Size S to M. INVISIBLE today (the flag is off), VISIBLE once live.

#### 9. Home page tiles promise things the pages do not have

- **Where:** `src/lib/nav.ts` lines 164 to 172.
- **What happens:** the Departures tile says "Departure times, gates and check-in": the board has no gates and no check-in information, and unlike the Arrivals tile it does not say "sample". The Getting Here tile says "Taxis, buses, car rental and parking": the page it opens (`content/travel/ground-transportation.mdx`) lists car rental firms only. Taxis are mentioned in one line of the frequently asked questions page and buses nowhere. The board page's search description (`src/app/flights/page.tsx` lines 9 to 11) says "Arrivals and departures for …" with no word that the schedule is a sample, so a search result promises a live board.
- **Rule or reference:** lane spec, Don't: "Present sample flight data without its label". Web-design-guidelines, Content and Copy: labels say what the person will get.
- **Why it matters:** honesty of the sample data, and a first-time visitor looking for a taxi fare lands on a page without one.
- **Suggested fix:** reword the two tiles and the search description now ("Sample departure board for MBIA and Lauriston"; "Car rental and parking"); ask the Authority for taxi fares, the bus route and the pick-up points, then restore the fuller wording. Size S for wording, M for the content. VISIBLE.

#### 10. The parking and transport pages are scraped text, not answers

- **Where:** `content/travel/parking-at-mbia.mdx`, `content/travel/ground-transportation.mdx`, `content/travel/passenger-information.mdx`, `content/at-the-airport/terminal-map.mdx`.
- **What happens:**
  - Parking: headings repeat ("Safe, secure spots", "Parking & Passenger Convenience", "Parking Rates", "Public Car Park Rates"), jump from the first level straight to the third, and use emoji as icons, which a screen reader reads aloud ("P button", "police car light") and which showed as empty boxes in the audit browser. The rates, the facts people came for, are at the bottom as bold sentences. "XCD $28.00 per calendar day (up to a maximum of eight hours per day)" cannot be understood. No opening hours, payment methods, map or walking time to check-in.
  - Ground transportation: six car rental firms with the same filler sentence each; phone numbers are plain text with no area code ("418-6500 439-4224"), so they cannot be tapped (staging: no `tel:` link on the page); several contacts are personal mail addresses carried over from the old site.
  - Passenger information is the old site's footer ("GAA, MBIA, Lauriston Airport, Useful Links, Newsletter") published as a page. A leftover "Newsletter" heading also ends 11 content files.
  - Terminal map has no map: the page has no image at all.
- **Rule or reference:** lane spec, Principles (task first) and Agent prompt guide 1 ("Parking & transport" interior page). Refactoring-ui, hierarchy and finishing touches. Fixing-accessibility: do not skip heading levels. Fly Delta's airport screen answers "distance from city centre" and "ground transportation options" in two short labelled blocks under a map; Fresha's "Getting there" block is a map, one line on parking, the address and "Get directions".
- **Why it matters:** this is the second journey. The information is thin, in the wrong order, and partly not there.
- **Suggested fix:** rewrite these four pages by hand and take them out of the converter's `PAGE_MAP` so they are not overwritten: rates as a small table at the top, then where the car park is, hours, payment and accessible bays; transport as taxi, bus, car rental blocks with tappable numbers; add the terminal map image or a plain "map coming" note with the floor-by-floor list; remove or redirect the passenger information page; strip the "Newsletter" headings in the converter. Needs facts from the Authority (open question 2 in the next-wave plan: who approves content). Size M to L. VISIBLE.

#### 11. Sea-blue small text on the pale bands and gold text on white are below the contrast limit

- **Where:** `src/app/page.tsx` lines 190 and 200 (the "At the Airport" and "News & Media" labels at 12 pixels and the "Everything at the airport" and "All news" links at 14 pixels, both sea blue `#0e7fa8` on the mist band `#f4f7fb`): 4.23 to 1, where small text needs 4.5. `src/app/not-found.tsx` line 7: "404 — Gate not found" in gold `#f0b429` on white: 1.86 to 1. On the board, the three-letter city code is white at 45 percent on navy: 4.41 to 1 at 12 pixels (`flight-board.tsx` line 217).
- **Rule or reference:** lane spec, Don't: "Use sea-bright or gold as text on white, or sea/muted text on tinted bands below AA."
- **Suggested fix:** on the mist band use navy for the label and link (the gold rule already carries the accent); on the not-found page use sea blue or navy for the label; raise the city code to white at 60 percent (6.9 to 1). Size S. INVISIBLE (slightly darker text), except the not-found label, which changes colour (VISIBLE, small).

#### 12. The contact form reports one problem at a time, at the bottom, and is small to tap

- **Where:** `src/components/contact-form.tsx` lines 33 to 38 and 74 to 118; `src/lib/contact.ts` lines 59 to 70.
- **What happens:** validation returns only the first problem as one line under the button; the field is not marked invalid, the message is not tied to it and focus does not move. The rules ("at least 10 characters") appear only after failing. The name and email fields have no `autocomplete`, and the email has no `spellCheck={false}`. On staging the fields and the Send button are 32 pixels tall on a phone and the message box is 64 pixels (two lines). The success line appears under the button while the form empties, with no focus move, so on a phone it can be below the fold of attention.
- **Rule or reference:** web-design-guidelines, Forms: errors inline next to fields, focus the first error, `autocomplete` and correct types. Fixing-accessibility, forms and errors: `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby`. Lane spec: tap targets of 44 pixels. The bookings audit ([bookings-2026-11.md](bookings-2026-11.md)) records the pattern the Bookings form already uses (summary that takes focus, per-field errors).
- **Suggested fix:** per-field errors with `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby`, focus the first invalid field, add `autocomplete="name"` and `autocomplete="email"`, make fields and the button 44 pixels tall and the message box at least five lines, and replace the form with a confirmation that takes focus and says what happens next. Size M. The attributes and focus are INVISIBLE; the sizes and confirmation are VISIBLE.

#### 13. Main buttons and links are still under 44 pixels on a phone

- **Where (measured on staging at 390 pixels, after #1732):** the two hero buttons and the two buttons in the business band (36 pixels, `src/app/page.tsx` lines 123 to 140 and 372 to 386); the gold Flight Information button in the phone menu (32 pixels, `src/components/mobile-nav-sheet.tsx` line 54, directly under a 44 pixel Call button); the "Plan your journey" links on the airport pages (32 pixels, `src/components/airport-page.tsx` line 84); both not-found buttons (32 pixels); "Sign in" (28 pixels); the section links beside headings (20 pixels); the "More in …" links on content pages (36 pixels, `src/app/[section]/[slug]/page.tsx` line 81); every footer link (17 to 20 pixels, `src/components/site-footer.tsx`).
- **Rule or reference:** lane spec, Accessibility: "tap targets ≥44px". #1732 fixed the menu button, menu links, desktop menu, board tabs and airport chips; these are what remains.
- **Suggested fix:** `min-h-11` on the listed buttons and links; give footer links vertical padding instead of spacing between them. Size S. Mostly INVISIBLE (hit area), but the hero and menu buttons grow a little (VISIBLE, small).

### Nice to have

#### 14. Cards and tiles give no pressed feedback on a phone

- **Where:** quick-task tiles, airport cards, experience cards, news cards and `PageCard` use hover lift only; the board tabs and chips change colour only after the tap completes. `app-feel.css` turns the tap flash off, and the review rules say every tappable control then needs its own pressed state.
- **Rule or reference:** emil-design-eng, "Buttons must feel responsive" (a slight scale on press); find-animation-opportunities. The lane spec says not to animate beyond hover lift, so keep this to a pressed state, not new motion.
- **Suggested fix:** `active:scale-[0.99]` (or a slightly darker border) on the card links, and an `active:` background on tabs and chips. Size S. VISIBLE only while pressing.

#### 15. Small motion details

- The image zoom on cards runs for 500 milliseconds with the default curve (`duration-500`, `page.tsx` lines 228 and 271, `airports/page.tsx`); review-animations asks for under 300 milliseconds and an ease-out curve for interface motion. Reduced motion is already honoured by `app-feel.css`.
- The menu chevron uses `transition` with `duration-300` on all properties (`site-header.tsx` line 69); name the property (`transition-transform`) and shorten to 150 to 200 milliseconds.
- The airline logos fade from 80 to 100 percent on hover but are not links (`page.tsx` line 305); remove the hover or link each logo to the airline section.
- Size S in total. VISIBLE (barely).

#### 16. The loading placeholder does not match the page it replaces

- **Where:** `src/app/loading.tsx`: white skeleton blocks in a narrower column, then the navy hero arrives, so the page flashes white to navy on every navigation.
- **Suggested fix:** start the placeholder with a navy band the height of `SectionHero`. Size S. VISIBLE.

#### 17. The error page is unstyled and the not-found page has the home title

- **Where:** `src/app/error.tsx` uses the default button and plain headings with none of the airport's type or colour; `src/app/not-found.tsx` has no title of its own, so the browser tab reads "Grenada Airports Authority — Gateway to the Spice of the Caribbean" on a missing page. The jokes ("This page seems to have departed", "Gate not found") are fine once, but a person who followed an old link from the previous website needs help: there is no search and no list of likely pages.
- **Suggested fix:** style the error page like the not-found page, give not-found the title "Page not found", and add four links (Flights, Parking, Getting here, Contact). Size S. VISIBLE.

#### 18. Wording and labels

- City and code say the same thing twice: "New York–JFK JFK", "London–Heathrow LHR" (`src/lib/flights.ts`). Use "New York" and "London Heathrow" with the code after.
- The empty board says "No arrivals listed for Lauriston today." while the schedule is a sample with no date (`flight-board.tsx` line 242). Say "No sample arrivals for Lauriston."
- The phone hides the Airline column and never shows the airline name (`flight-board.tsx` lines 172 and 222); put the airline under the city in small text on phones.
- Page titles are in mixed styles: "Parking At MBIA", "Check in Procedures", "AIRPORT LOUNGES", "IAM JET CENTRE LOUNGE" (content front matter). Pick sentence case.
- Every section page has the same label above the title, "Grenada Airports Authority", instead of saying where you are; content pages do link back to their section.
- "MBIA" appears on chips and in page titles without ever being spelled out next to them for a first-time visitor.
- Size S in total. VISIBLE.

#### 19. Board colours still use raw palette values

- **Where:** `flight-board.tsx` lines 17 to 25 (`text-emerald-300`, `text-sky-300`, `text-amber-300`, `text-red-300`). The lane spec already lists this as known debt "to replace with `gaa` tokens when touched"; the on-navy status text colours need naming as tokens. "Scheduled", "Departed" and "Landed" also share one grey style, so a finished flight and one not yet started look the same at a glance. Size S, needs token names in the pull request. VISIBLE (slight).

#### 20. Airport weather is missing

- **Where:** no page or panel anywhere in `apps/web/mbia` mentions current weather, although the Meteorological Service is the Authority's own department and its observations for the airport station are already published on the weather site.
- **Why it matters:** "is it raining at the airport, is there a warning in force" sits right beside "is the flight on time", for both the traveller and the person collecting them.
- **Suggested fix:** a small "Weather at the airport" strip under the board (conditions in words, temperature, wind, time of the observation, any warning in force) linking to the weather site for the rest, fed through FastAPI like the board, with the same honesty rules: show the observation time and say so when the reading is old. This is a new feature and crosses two client departments, so it needs an owner decision first. Size L. VISIBLE.

## Inspiration

Ideas only. The site keeps the airport's own navy, sea and gold look; nothing here is a reason to copy another brand's layout or colours.

**Flight board**

- [Google Maps, station departures](https://mobbin.com/screens/817f224d-319c-4a04-9302-d4e82611dc0b): each row is destination, then "On time · 7:52" in green words or "Delayed 3 min" in red words with the old time struck through, and the time that matters large on the right. Borrow the row anatomy for the phone board (finding 7). Do not borrow the countdown in minutes: flights are read by clock time.
- [Transit, departures for one line](https://mobbin.com/screens/bfc8b4f1-09b1-410b-86f0-173422392314): live times carry a small signal mark and scheduled times are plain grey, so "real" and "timetable" are told apart without a word. Borrow the idea of a visible difference between live and sample or scheduled rows. Do not borrow the full-colour background.

**One flight's status**

- [Singapore Airlines, Flight Status](https://mobbin.com/flows/cef2ab6f-7553-44c9-8d01-1046272a36eb) and its [result screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/19978438-40f3-4262-a0fb-7b63f3269fc9): search by route or flight number, then one card with the status word and icon, "Scheduled" small above "Estimated" large, the terminal, and "Last updated 12:48, 8 Feb 2024". Borrow the scheduled-above-estimated pair and the last-updated line for a tap-to-open row. Do not borrow "Following" (it needs accounts and notifications).
- [American Airlines, delayed trip](https://mobbin.com/screens/e8379c38-2419-46f2-b83f-403bd8d00269): a plain-language banner says why the flight is late, new times are in the warning colour beside the grey scheduled ones, and terminal, gate and baggage belt sit underneath. Borrow the one-sentence reason and the baggage belt for arrivals. Do not borrow the booking details; an airport does not hold them.
- [American Airlines, Flight status entry](https://mobbin.com/flows/839a28ff-e2ef-4ece-a02b-5fedc6028b59): "Flight status" is the first of three round shortcuts on the home screen, and the search has two tabs, city or flight number. Borrow the shortcut position for the phone home page (finding 3).

**Airport information: parking, transport, map**

- [Fly Delta, airport information](https://mobbin.com/screens/63d05eda-3a07-4136-a493-35d962a28089): four icon tabs (flights, ground transport, food, weather) over a map, then short labelled blocks: "Distance from city center", "Ground transportation options". Borrow the labelled-block answer format for the parking and transport pages (finding 10) and the idea that weather belongs beside transport. Do not borrow icon-only tabs; label them.

- **Production.** Only staging was visited. Staging already has the Call button and the 44 pixel menu and board targets, so it includes #1694 and #1732.
- **Slow connections and print.** Not throttled; the lane spec's "printable" rule was not tested with a print preview.
