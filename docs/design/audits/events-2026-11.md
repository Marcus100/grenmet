# Design audit: Barrels Events (2026-11)

**Status:** Phase 1 audit, read only. Nothing was changed in the app; every fix below is a proposal for the owner.  
**Owner:** Barrels Grenada engineering (design mastery programme, issue #1453, section 8)  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-23

## Scope and method

Surfaces audited, all in `apps/web/events`:

- the public site: home, calendar (list and month), the event page with the ticket block, groups, the organiser page, profiles, the season page, Suggest an event, Privacy, and the not-found and error pages;
- the member pages: My plans, My tickets, the ticket page with its QR code, the order page, Network, Messages and the welcome questions;
- the organiser console (`/dash`): overview, My events, the event editor, Orders and one order, Attendees, Door, Outlet sales, Settlement, and Team and roles.

The rule for judging: a defect is a gap against this app's own lane spec ([`docs/design/events.md`](../events.md)) or a rule from a design skill, never a difference from another app.

Method:

1. Read the instructions (`AGENTS.md` at the root and in `apps/web/events`), the lane spec, and the section "Reviewing or polishing UI: check these before reporting a gap" in `docs/agents/extended-rules.md`.
2. Checked where styles live before reporting anything. The shared layer `packages/ui/src/styles/app-feel.css` is imported by `src/app/globals.css` and already switches off the tap flash, sets `touch-action: manipulation` on controls, stops a long press selecting button text, contains scrolling inside dialogs and cancels animation for people who ask for less motion. The shared button (`packages/ui/src/components/ui/button.tsx`) has a pressed state and a focus ring, and Tailwind applies `hover:` styles only on devices that can hover. Nothing here repeats a gap those files already cover.
3. Read every page and shared component listed above, then applied the rules of these skills: ui-polish-pass (states, widths, touch, token discipline), web-design-guidelines, fixing-accessibility, mobile-native, emil-design-eng, break-ui (worst-case data, argued from the code), review-animations and the repo's refactoring-ui. The app has almost no motion (colour changes and a two-pixel lift on cards), so the motion review found nothing to report.
4. Looked at the running staging app (`events.staging.barrels.gd`) with a scripted browser at 390 pixels (phone, touch emulation) and 1280 pixels (desktop), signed out and signed in as the staging test account. The script measured tap target sizes, text sizes, sideways overflow, heading order and console errors on 12 public and 16 signed-in screens at each width. Nothing was bought, submitted, issued or deleted. The screenshots were viewed during the audit and are not committed.
5. Left out everything already listed in issue #1688 and fixed by pull requests #1695 and #1697 (the phone buy bar, the unexplained disabled button, the phone menu links, the share link, the empty "Groups you might like", EC$ in orders, and the active ticket count).

How to read a finding:

- **Rule or reference** is the lane spec line or skill rule behind it.
- **Size:** S is under half a day, M is about a day, L is several days or needs a decision first.
- **INVISIBLE** means visitors and organisers would not see a difference in normal use (accessibility, focus, keyboard, hit areas, behaviour on failure). These can merge on green checks. **VISIBLE** means layout, colour or wording changes, so the owner reviews before and after screenshots first.

## What is already strong

- **The event leads.** Big display type, category-toned flyer panels and a white date stamp, exactly as the lane spec asks; one first-level heading on every page measured, and no skipped heading level on any of them.
- **Tokens are respected.** No hard-coded colours in components (the QR code's black on white is deliberate), no custom animation, no stray easing or duration values, and one type scale almost everywhere.
- **No sideways scroll** at 390 pixels on 27 of the 28 screens measured (the exception is finding 7), and no console errors apart from the expected 404 on the not-found page.
- **Money and time are consistent for buyers.** Prices read `EC$10.00`, times say "Grenada time", and counts use tabular figures.
- **Buying is careful.** A double press cannot make two orders, the button says what is missing instead of sitting disabled without a reason, the hold on reserved tickets is explained with its end time, refund terms are shown before paying, and results are announced to screen readers.
- **Failure is handled in words.** Server refusals show the server's own sentence, an uncertain money action locks the form until the organiser has checked, and every final step in the console (cancel event, cancel order, remove a member) goes through a confirm dialog that says what will happen.
- **The console is honest.** Disabled navigation items say why ("Pick an event", "Ticketed events only", "Coming soon"), empty lists say what to do next, and settlement puts discrepancies first.
- **Labels are real.** Every form field measured has a label; icon-only buttons have names ("Fewer Test ticket tickets", "Save Staging test event").

## Findings

### Blocking

#### 1. At the door, "admitted" and "refused" look almost the same

- **Where:** `src/components/dash/door-console.tsx` lines 131 to 144. A good scan shows a box with a hibiscus (pink-red) border and hibiscus text; a refused scan shows a box with a red border and red text. The good scan has a small tick and the text "Name · ticket type · code"; it never says the word "Admitted". The refused one has no icon.
- **Rule or reference:** lane spec, "Colour has jobs. Hibiscus = act" and "Status colours stay shared and are not overridden". Refactoring-ui: colour must never be the only signal. The same pattern (success written in hibiscus beside errors in red) is in `ticket-claim.tsx` line 337, `event-lifecycle.tsx` lines 31 and 185, and `order-actions.tsx` line 165.
- **Why it matters:** this is the one screen read in half a second, at night, on a phone, with a queue. Two red-family boxes of the same shape make it easy to wave in a ticket that was refused ("Already admitted at 9:14 PM by…") or to turn away a good one.
- **Suggested fix:** use the shared success colour (green) with a tick and the word "Admitted" for a good scan, the shared error colour with a cross and "Not admitted" for a refusal, and make the box larger than the rest of the page's text. Apply the shared success colour to the other four places. Size S. VISIBLE.

### Should fix

#### 2. On a phone the ticket chooser starts below the screen, under a bar whose button does nothing yet

- **Where:** `src/app/(public)/events/[slug]/page.tsx` (the buy card is the second column, so on a phone it comes after the cover, the facts and the whole description) and `src/components/discovery/ticket-claim.tsx` lines 54 to 61 (the fixed bar). On staging at 390 by 844 the bar covers the screen from 734 pixels down and the first ticket row starts at 875 pixels, even with a two-line description. The bar's button reads "Choose how many tickets" and is disabled.
- **Rule or reference:** lane spec, "Phone first on the public site". Mobile-native: put the choice in reach of a thumb. Ui-polish-pass: no dead control on the first screen.
- **Why it matters:** the first thing a buyer taps is the big button, and nothing happens. With a real description the plus and minus buttons can be several screens down.
- **Suggested fix:** while nothing is chosen, make the bar's button live and scroll to the ticket rows (label "Choose tickets"); or move the buy card above "About" on phones. Size S. VISIBLE.

#### 3. A result or error after pressing the buy button can land under the fixed bar

- **Where:** `ticket-claim.tsx` line 111: `scrollIntoView({ block: "nearest" })` brings the notice to the bottom edge of the screen, which is where the 98 pixel bar sits. The notice has no bottom scroll margin. Read from code; it could not be triggered without buying.
- **Rule or reference:** fixing-accessibility, announcements and errors must be perceivable; ui-polish-pass, error state.
- **Why it matters:** "Sold out", "Reserved, awaiting payment" and "You have 2 new tickets" are the answers to the most important press on the site.
- **Suggested fix:** give the notice a bottom scroll margin equal to the bar's height on phones, or show the notice inside the bar. Size S. INVISIBLE.

#### 4. The controls people press most are 28 to 36 pixels tall

- **Where (measured at 390 pixels):** ticket plus and minus 32 by 32 (`ticket-claim.tsx` lines 187 and 202); header "Sign in" 67 by 32 and menu button 36 by 36 (`site-chrome.tsx`, `nav-link.tsx` line 88); the home page shortcut chips 32 tall and "See all" 21 tall (`(public)/page.tsx`); calendar List, Month and Suggest 32 to 36 tall; filter fields 40 tall (`filter-bar.tsx`); footer links 21 tall; every field on Suggest an event 32 tall; "Follow for new events" 36; "Show QR code", "Print" and "Cancel ticket" 32 (`me/tickets`); the My plans tabs 25 tall; Report and Block 28 (`connect-actions.tsx`); Accept, Decline and Remove 28 (`network-actions.tsx`); in the console, Admit 32 and the per-row Admit 28 (`door-console.tsx` line 199), "Sell and print" and "Close till" 32, every editor field 32, Save draft and Publish 36; the close button of both menus 28 by 28 (shared sheet).
- **Rule or reference:** lane spec, "44px+ targets" on the public site. The console is desktop-first, but Door and Outlet sales are used standing up with a phone.
- **Why it matters:** the two controls that sell a ticket are among the smallest on the page; at the door a 28 pixel row button admits the wrong person with one slip, and there is no undo.
- **Suggested fix:** one pass that gives public controls and the Door and Outlet screens a 44 pixel minimum (the pattern already used for the onboarding chips and the console navigation, `min-h-11`), with padding on inline links. Size M. INVISIBLE for the hit areas that grow by padding; the taller buttons and fields are VISIBLE.

#### 5. Search and filter fields zoom the page on an iPhone

- **Where:** the home search (`(public)/page.tsx` line 72) and the calendar search (`filter-bar.tsx` line 49) are 14 pixels; every select (calendar filters, Suggest an event, the event editor) is 14 pixels from the shared `native-select.tsx`. The shared text input is already 16 pixels on phones.
- **Rule or reference:** mobile-native, "Page zooms into the input": text under 16 pixels in a field makes iOS zoom and stay zoomed.
- **Suggested fix:** 16 pixels on phones for these fields (`text-base md:text-sm`, as the shared input does). The select change belongs in `packages/ui` and affects other apps, so it needs a check there. Size S. INVISIBLE on desktop, slightly larger text on phones.

#### 6. Blocking a person, and removing a connection, happen on one tap with no confirmation

- **Where:** `src/components/community/connect-actions.tsx` line 130 (Block, a 28 pixel button directly beside Report) and `network-actions.tsx` (Remove). Block runs at once and sends the member to their network page.
- **Rule or reference:** lane spec, "Social is safe by default". Web-design-guidelines: destructive actions need a confirmation or an undo. Every destructive step in the console already has one.
- **Why it matters:** a slip while reaching for Report blocks someone, and nothing on the screen says how to reverse it.
- **Suggested fix:** a confirm dialog for Block that says what blocking does and how to unblock; an undo line after Remove. Size S. VISIBLE.

#### 7. My plans scrolls sideways on a phone

- **Where:** `src/app/(public)/me/page.tsx` line 42: My tickets, Edit interests, View profile and Sign out sit in a row that cannot wrap. Measured: the page is 420 pixels wide in a 390 pixel screen and "Sign out" is cut off.
- **Rule or reference:** ui-polish-pass, widths: no horizontal page scroll.
- **Suggested fix:** let the row wrap, or keep My tickets as the one button and move the other three into a menu. Size S. VISIBLE.

#### 8. The organiser overview breaks at phone width

- **Where:** `src/components/dash/organiser-overview.tsx`. The three sales tiles (line 216) are fixed at three columns, so "EC$0.00" already runs outside its tile; a real total such as EC$12,450.00 will run further. The Settlement table (line 151) has six columns and no scrolling wrapper, so "Admitted" and "Cash" touch and the cash value collides with the Issues count (it reads "EC$0.000").
- **Rule or reference:** events `AGENTS.md`, the console is desktop-first "with a usable responsive summary". Break-ui: never squeeze or truncate amounts.
- **Suggested fix:** one column of tiles on phones, and either the scrolling wrapper the other console tables use or a stacked row per event. Size S. VISIBLE.

#### 9. Ticket types cannot be edited on a phone

- **Where:** `src/components/dash/event-editor.tsx` line 336: the row is `name, 6rem, 6rem, 6rem, button`. At 390 pixels the name field is 39 pixels wide. From the second row on, the column labels are hidden, so three number boxes sit side by side with nothing to say which is price, allocation or limit.
- **Rule or reference:** as finding 8; fixing-accessibility, visible labels.
- **Why it matters:** correcting a price or adding a ticket type from a phone on event day is a likely task.
- **Suggested fix:** stack each ticket type as a small card with labelled fields under the laptop width. Size M. VISIBLE.

#### 10. Save and Publish sit at the very end of a long editor

- **Where:** `event-editor.tsx` line 465. The "Ready to publish?" card is a side column that sticks only from the widest breakpoint; on a phone the editor is 3,649 pixels tall and the buttons come after the preview. There is no warning when leaving with unsaved changes.
- **Rule or reference:** refactoring-ui, keep the primary action with the work; web-design-guidelines, warn before losing unsaved changes.
- **Suggested fix:** a sticky bottom bar on smaller screens with the count of things still missing, Save draft and Publish; a leave-page warning while the draft differs from what was saved. Size M. VISIBLE.

#### 11. Not-found and error pages have no header, footer or way around

- **Where:** `src/app/not-found.tsx` and `src/app/error.tsx`. Their comments say they render "inside the root layout, so site navigation stays usable", but the header and footer live in `(public)/layout.tsx`, not the root layout. On staging `/no-such-page` and a missing event show a heading, one line and a 32 pixel button on an otherwise empty page, with no main landmark. They also use raw sizes (`text-2xl`, `text-sm`) instead of the lane's display type.
- **Rule or reference:** lane spec, Type; fixing-accessibility, landmarks.
- **Why it matters:** an old event link shared on WhatsApp is the most likely way to land here, and the page does not look like Barrels Events.
- **Suggested fix:** render both inside the public shell with the wordmark, a search box and "Browse events"; for a missing event say "This event is no longer listed". Size S. VISIBLE.

#### 12. Home hides "Tonight" and "Don't miss" when they are empty

- **Where:** `src/app/(public)/page.tsx` lines 117 and 173. "This weekend" and "Groups you might like" show an empty state; these two disappear.
- **Rule or reference:** owner rule, never auto-hide an empty section. Lane spec gives lime the job of "happening now", and that job is absent from the home page on most days.
- **Suggested fix:** always show "Tonight" with a line such as "Nothing on tonight. Next up: Saturday" and a link, and show "Don't miss" with the next few events when none is featured. Size S. VISIBLE.

#### 13. Saving and copying fail silently, and keyboard users cannot skip the header

- **Where and what:**
  - `save-button.tsx` line 29: a failed save shows nothing (the error from `useMemberAction` is dropped).
  - `share-actions.tsx` line 28: a failed copy shows nothing; a successful one changes the label to "Copied" for good and is not announced.
  - `suggest-form.tsx` line 76 and the welcome form: the form is replaced by the thank-you panel and keyboard focus is lost; a status region added at the same moment as its text is often not read out.
  - No "Skip to content" link anywhere; the first stop is the wordmark, then the whole header on every page.
  - `connect-actions.tsx` line 112: why "Message" is disabled is only a hover tooltip, which touch and keyboard users never see.
  - `month-grid.tsx`: on phones each event in the month view is a dot 38 by 28 pixels, and each day is announced as a raw date ("2026-12-12: 1 events").
- **Rule or reference:** fixing-accessibility (announcements, focus, keyboard access); web-design-guidelines (feedback for every action).
- **Suggested fix:** show the error under the control; reset "Copied" after two seconds inside a live region; move focus to the confirmation; add the skip link and a main target; put the reason as visible text; make each day with events one link to that day's list, named in words. Size M in total. INVISIBLE except the visible reason text and the month-view day links.

### Polish

#### 14. The price is written twice on a phone, in two formats, above a large empty band

- The fixed bar says "From EC$10.00" and the card heading just above the ticket rows says "From EC$10" (`priceLabel` and `formatPrice` disagree). On the event page the bottom padding is applied twice (`events/[slug]/page.tsx` line 82 and `buy-bar-space.ts`), which leaves about 290 pixels of blank page before the footer. Size S. VISIBLE.

#### 15. Money wording in the console

- `order-detail.tsx` line 76 heads the section "Money (XCD)"; the owner's rule is EC$. `organiser-overview.tsx` lines 177 and 225 assume the currency instead of reading it. Size S. VISIBLE.

#### 16. Names for the same thing differ

- The navigation says "Door & box office" and "Tickets & capacity"; the editor's buttons say "Door" and "Outlet sales"; the page titles are "Door" and "Outlet sales". The navigation says "Build event", the pages say "Create an event" and "Customise event". Pick one word for each (suggest "Door", "Box office", "Edit event"). The five buttons at the top of the editor repeat the navigation and can go once the names match. Size S. VISIBLE.

#### 17. Small type carries essential facts

- The lane's caption size is 12 pixels and it is used for the date and time on cards, the venue, the price, "20 left" and the refund terms. Nothing is smaller than 12 pixels on the website (the 10 pixel size is only in the installed-app tab bar). Consider 13 or 14 pixels for the date, venue and price lines on phones. Needs a lane spec change first. Size S. VISIBLE.

#### 18. Long content is not guarded

- Read from code, not seen: a message bubble has no word breaking (`messages/[threadId]/page.tsx` line 78), so a long link pushes the bubble past the screen; the event title, organiser name heading and ticket type name wrap only at spaces. Add `overflow-wrap: anywhere` to those. The message box on a phone floats 80 pixels above the bottom (`message-composer.tsx` line 40), a gap meant for the installed app's tab bar that the website does not have. Size S. INVISIBLE (the composer position is VISIBLE).

#### 19. Thin empty states on member pages

- "No plans yet." (`me/page.tsx` line 81), "No meetups scheduled yet.", an empty Members tab with nothing at all (`groups/[slug]/page.tsx` line 164), an "About" heading over an empty line on a profile with no bio (`people/[handle]/page.tsx` line 97), and My tickets, which drops the "Upcoming" heading when only past tickets exist. Each should keep its section and say what to do next with a link. Size S. VISIBLE.

#### 20. Past events are faded below the contrast limit

- `o/[slug]/page.tsx` line 91 sets the whole past-events grid to 80 percent opacity, which takes the grey caption text from 6.6 to about 4.1 against the paper (the standard needs 4.5 for small text). Label them "Ended" instead of fading them. Size S. VISIBLE.

#### 21. Smaller items

- `sign-in/page.tsx` line 27 uses a size class that does not exist (`text-heading-2`), so the fallback sign-in heading renders at body size. INVISIBLE in normal use (the page normally redirects).
- Group cards use a second-level heading inside a section that already has one (`group-card.tsx` line 32); make them third-level. INVISIBLE.
- The role dialog speaks of "23:59 UTC" (`grant-role-dialog.tsx` line 242); say "7:59 pm Grenada time" only. VISIBLE.
- The Door page puts the cursor in the code field on load, which opens the phone keyboard over the counts; do that only when a hardware scanner or a wide screen is likely. VISIBLE on phones.
- The home search placeholder is cut off at 390 pixels ("Search fetes, meetups, venu"); shorten it to "Search events". VISIBLE.
- The ticket page shows the QR code at up to 256 pixels with no hint to turn the screen brightness up; a one-line hint helps at a dark gate. VISIBLE.
- The last-resort error page (`global-error.tsx`) is unstyled. VISIBLE only in a rare failure.
- Number fields in the editor snap an emptied box back to 0, so a price cannot be cleared before typing. Let the box be empty while it has focus. INVISIBLE.

## What could not be checked

- **A real phone.** Tap delay, sticky hover, the on-screen keyboard, the browser bar over the fixed buy bar and the notch were argued from the stylesheets and an emulated phone only.
- **Buying a ticket.** Nothing was bought or claimed, so the redirect to payment, "Reserved, awaiting payment", the new-tickets message, the order page and a ticket page with a real QR code were audited from code only. The test account holds no tickets.
- **Groups, profiles, network rows, messages and the organiser page.** Staging has no groups, connections or message threads, so those screens were seen only empty or read from code. Block, Report and the message box were not pressed.
- **A busy site.** Staging has one event. Cards in a full grid, a month view with many events, long titles and non-Latin names were argued from code, not seen.
- **Door and Outlet in use.** The screens were opened but no ticket was admitted, no till opened and nothing sold, so the result box, the camera scanner and the printed slip were read from code. The camera scanner relies on a browser feature that iPhones do not have; that is a recorded decision and is only noted here.
- **Other roles.** Only the test account's organiser role was seen; the no-access, awaiting-verification and "Events is switched off" states were read from code.
- **Screen readers and the full keyboard route:** reasoned from the markup, not run with a screen reader.
- **Slow connections.** Pages loaded in 1.3 to 3.2 seconds on staging; no throttled run was made, so the loading skeleton was read from code only.
- **Reference flows.** No Mobbin comparison was made in this pass; issue #1688 already carries the references for the buy bar and the event page.
- **Production and the sign-in app.** Only staging was visited, and the Barrels sign-in screens belong to the auth app's own audit.
