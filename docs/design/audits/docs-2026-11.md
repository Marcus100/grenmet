# Design audit: public guides, docs.barrels.gd (2026-11)

**Status:** Phase 1 audit, read only. Nothing was changed in the app; every fix below is a proposal for the owner.  
**Owner:** Barrels Grenada engineering (design mastery programme, issue #1453)  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-23

## Scope and method

Surfaces audited, all in `apps/web/docs` (docs.barrels.gd, Next.js with Markdown content): the catalogue home page, the staff guide, the Tropical Cyclone Emergency Plan chapters (pre-season, warning issued, during and after the storm, appendices), the search dialog and search page, the page footer and feedback box, the sign-in button, and the not-found page.

**There is no lane spec for this app** (`docs/design/docs.md` does not exist; the app notes in `apps/web/docs/AGENTS.md` cover code only). That is itself a finding (finding 7). The app is judged against [`docs/design/gms.md`](../gms.md), the nearest lane (the content belongs to the Grenada Meteorological Service and the Grenada Airports Authority), and against the rules of the design skills.

The questions asked: can a staff member find the right guide quickly, and can someone read it on a phone, perhaps with weak signal and low battery, before a storm?

Method:

1. Read the root and app `AGENTS.md`, the GMS lane spec and the section "Reviewing or polishing UI: check these before reporting a gap". Skipped what is in issue 1688 and what pull request 1694 (docs search page, tap targets) and 1732 (footer, mobile navigation and navigation links) already fixed.
2. Checked where styles live. `src/styles/tailwind.css` imports the shared `app-feel.css`, so tap flash, `touch-action` and the CSS reduced-motion override are in place. The JavaScript `motion` library used in the header and navigation is **not** covered by that CSS (finding 6).
3. Read the layout, header, navigation, footer, search, feedback, not-found page and the cyclone plan pages, then applied web-design-guidelines, fixing-accessibility, mobile-native, refactoring-ui, emil-design-eng, break-ui, review-animations and find-animation-opportunities.
4. Looked at the live production site (read only; the feedback box was not used) with a scripted browser at 390 pixels (touch emulation) and 1280 pixels, light and dark colour preference, on the home page, the "during the hurricane or storm" page and a missing page.
5. Compared with two saved reference screens (see Inspiration).

How to read a finding: **Size** S is under half a day, M about a day, L several days or needs a decision. **INVISIBLE** means readers would not see a difference in normal use; **VISIBLE** means layout, colour or wording changes, so the owner reviews it first.

## What is already strong

- **Short pages that answer one question.** The storm pages are 15 to 25 lines each, with a lead sentence and an action checklist at the top.
- **Findability tools exist.** A search dialog, a search page, a left navigation with sections, "Previous" and "Next" links at the foot of each page, and a catalogue home page.
- **Accessibility groundwork.** A skip link first in the page, 44 pixel minimum height on navigation links and footer links (a test enforces the footer), `aria-current` on the active page, a labelled previous and next button.
- **Honest about personal data.** The emergency personnel appendix explains why phone numbers are not published and where staff get the list.
- **Anonymous, light feedback.** "Was this page helpful?" sends no personal data.
- **Light mobile weight.** Static content, no horizontal scroll at 390 pixels on the pages seen.

## Findings

### Blocking

#### 1. On a phone the "Emergency contacts" button disappears, and the page it opens holds no contact details

- **Where:** `src/components/Header.tsx` lines 99 to 105: the button is wrapped in `hidden min-[416px]:contents`, so it exists only at 416 pixels and wider. A 390 pixel phone (most iPhones) never sees it. The target, `src/content/appendix/emergency-personnel.mdx`, lists only roles ("Duty meteorologists and forecasters", "Airport duty managers") and says the phone numbers are kept off the website.
- **What happens:** during a storm the one shortcut that says "emergency" is missing on the devices people will use, and on a laptop it leads to a table of job titles with no name, number or radio channel. The page tells the reader to ask the duty office, which may be the person who cannot be reached.
- **Rule or reference:** fixing-accessibility and mobile-native (the main action must be reachable at the smallest width); Lemonade's checklist and Messenger's checkup (see Inspiration) put the one urgent action in view, not behind a width rule.
- **Why it matters:** this site is most used when time is short and signal is poor.
- **Suggested fix:** show the shortcut at every width (an icon button or a bar under the header on hurricane pages). What goes behind it is an owner decision: the numbers must not be public, so either a sign-in-only page for staff (the sign-in already exists, ADR-0017) or a clear "who to call, in order, and what to do if nobody answers" using only public switchboard numbers. Size S for the button, M to L for the content. VISIBLE.

### Should fix

#### 2. Nothing on the home page answers "a storm is coming, what do I do now?"

- **Where:** `src/content/index.mdx`: a catalogue of documents and a sentence "For cyclone preparedness and response, open the Tropical Cyclone Emergency Plan". The plan's own index (`hurricane-plan.mdx`) leads with an introduction.
- **What happens:** a person who opens the site at the first warning must pick the plan, then work out which of Pre-season, Warning issued, During and After applies, and which department. There is no "current phase" choice and no one-tap path per role.
- **Rule or reference:** refactoring-ui, hierarchy (the urgent task should be the biggest thing); the Tripadvisor help-centre home (see Inspiration) puts search and the choose-by-topic lists above everything.
- **Suggested fix:** at the top of the plan page add four large cards in order (Before the season, Warning issued, During the storm, After the storm), and on "Warning issued" a list of departments (it already exists in navigation). Do not hide any empty chapter (owner rule). Size M. VISIBLE.

#### 3. No print layout and no way to read it with the internet down

- **Where:** no `print:` styles anywhere in `src` (searched), and `public/` holds only favicons: no manifest and no service worker.
- **What happens:** in a storm, power and mobile data fail first. A staff member cannot save the plan on their phone for offline reading, and printing the page includes the navigation and search bar, then cuts the tables.
- **Rule or reference:** mobile-native (installable, works on weak signal); the emergency-checklist references saved on the phone itself.
- **Suggested fix:** (a) a print stylesheet that drops the header, left navigation and feedback box and keeps the checklists and tables (S, VISIBLE only in print); (b) a "Save for offline" for the plan using a small cache, with "last updated" shown on every page (M to L; needs an owner decision because it adds a service worker). INVISIBLE when not printing.

#### 4. Phone reading of the storm pages: dense navigation, small secondary text and no "last updated"

- **Where:** `src/components/Navigation.tsx` (long left list that opens as a full-screen menu on a phone), `Search.tsx` (the result path line is `text-2xs`, 12 pixels, at `text-zinc-500` on white) and on the pages read: no revision date or version, although the plan is labelled "(2024)".
- **What happens:** a reader cannot tell whether the checklist is current. The navigation lists about 40 pages in groups, so finding "Warning issued, Security" takes several scrolls. The grey 12 pixel path text in search results is the faintest text on the site (contrast was not measured).
- **Rule or reference:** web-design-guidelines (readable sizes), break-ui (many pages), fixing-accessibility (contrast).
- **Suggested fix:** show "Reviewed [date], plan year" under the title on every plan page; make the mobile menu groups collapsible with the current group open; raise the search path text to 14 pixels in the stronger grey. Size M. VISIBLE.

#### 5. Share previews are missing and the site name says "GMS" on a Barrels address

- **Where:** `src/app/layout.tsx` metadata: a title template "%s - GMS Documentation" and no Open Graph tags, images or per-page descriptions in the head (the content files have descriptions, but they are not wired to `openGraph`). The 404 page and the hero use the generic pattern.
- **What happens:** a link to the storm checklist in a WhatsApp group shows a bare address. The brand is the Met Office documentation library on a `barrels.gd` domain; an outsider cannot tell who runs it.
- **Rule or reference:** web-design-guidelines, Metadata; the client rule that the client's marks stay the client's and the software credit is Barrels.
- **Suggested fix:** wire the page description to Open Graph and add one share image (the plan title on navy) per section; keep "Grenada Meteorological Service" as the library name and add a small "Run on Barrels" credit in the footer if the owner agrees. Size S to M. VISIBLE in shares.

#### 6. Page motion ignores the reduced-motion setting

- **Where:** `Header.tsx` (scroll-linked background) and `Navigation.tsx` (`AnimatePresence`, `motion`). No `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"` in the app (searched), while the rules file says GMS, GAA Admin and Elections wrap their apps in it. `app-feel.css` covers CSS animation only.
- **Rule or reference:** `docs/agents/extended-rules.md` motion library note; fixing-accessibility (respect reduced motion); review-animations.
- **Suggested fix:** wrap the provider tree in `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"`. Size S. INVISIBLE.

#### 7. No lane spec for this app

- **Where:** `docs/design/` has `gms.md`, `barrels.md`, `commit.md` and others but no `docs.md`; the app is described only in code. The layout forces light colours (`style={{ colorScheme: "light" }}`) while many `dark:` classes remain in the components, and there is no theme switch to turn them on, so a reader on a dark phone at night in a power cut gets a white screen.
- **Why it matters:** with no spec, reviewers cannot say which look is intended (GMS navy, the Tailwind template look, Barrels), and the dead `dark:` styles tempt later edits.
- **Suggested fix:** write a short `docs/design/docs.md` (palette from the GMS tokens, heading scale, the storm-page pattern, dark mode yes or no) and either wire a manual dark switch like GMS or remove the dead `dark:` classes. Size S for the spec. VISIBLE once dark mode is decided.

### Nice to have

#### 8. Developer-style pages in the staff guide

Pages such as authentication, webhooks, SDKs, pagination and messages under "Staff guide" sound like a developer API reference, which does not match a Met Office documentation library. Review which belong in public guides (a content decision for the owner). Size S. VISIBLE.

#### 9. The not-found page offers search but not the storm shortcut

`not-found.tsx` has a search box and "Back to docs". Add the four storm phase links so a mistyped address in a storm still leads somewhere useful. Size S. VISIBLE.

#### 10. Feedback after a storm

"Was this page helpful?" has no free-text field; add an optional sentence ("What was missing?") that goes with the answer, so after an event the owners can see what failed. Size S to M. VISIBLE.

#### 11. Long table cells on a phone

The tables in `pre-season/general.mdx` and `appendix/mbia-vehicles.mdx` were checked only at the page level; wide tables should scroll inside their own box (break-ui). Size S. INVISIBLE.

## Inspiration

Ideas only; nothing here is to be copied.

- [Tripadvisor Forums, home](https://mobbin.com/screens/250e7273-9672-4203-96ba-ed3fc45f726b): one search field at the top and two plain lists underneath ("Browse by destination", "Browse by theme"). Borrow the two-list shape for the catalogue home (by document, by phase of the storm). Do not borrow the sign-in greeting.
- [Remote, help assistant with related articles](https://mobbin.com/screens/67f953a4-0f17-4d66-845e-a852f336c3de): an answer with a short "Related articles" list at the foot and a one-tap thumbs-up feedback. Borrow the "Related" list of two or three links on each storm page. Do not borrow the chat assistant.
- [Lemonade Insurance, "Your home" checklist](https://mobbin.com/screens/50e3f21d-713b-4c02-8990-fb4d3b6b0d50): large tick rows with an icon each and one big "Next" button in reach of the thumb. Borrow it for the action checklists (ticks the reader can set while working through the pre-season list, kept on the phone only). Do not borrow the pink tint or the sprinkler icons.
- [Messenger, Security checkup](https://mobbin.com/screens/957ed3f6-c114-452a-b1bf-84deaa9a0feb): a status line ("1 action to complete") above a "Recommended" and an "Up-to-date" group. Borrow the idea of a status line at the top of a plan page. Do not borrow the illustration.

## What could not be checked

- **A real phone, weak signal and a battery saver.** Everything was seen in an emulated phone viewport at normal speed.
- **Measured tap targets and contrast.** A scripted measurement run was queued behind other agents' work on the shared heavy-job slot and stopped, so the sizes and the grey text contrast are argued from the code; run a contrast tool before finding 4 is acted on.
- **The signed-in site.** The sign-in switch needs a client secret; only the signed-out production pages were seen.
- **The search dialog results and the feedback box.** The dialog was read from code; the feedback box was not used so no feedback was recorded.
- **Every chapter.** The home, one storm page and the not-found page were looked at; the other 30 chapters were checked only for shared layout and tables by listing the content files.
- **Dark mode.** The page is forced to light, so the dark styles could not be seen.
- **A screen reader.** Reasoned from the markup.
