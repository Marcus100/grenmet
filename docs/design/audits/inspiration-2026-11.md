# Design inspiration: eight audited apps (2026-11)

**Status:** Ideas only, read only. Nothing was changed in any app; every reference is a suggestion for the owner and for whoever builds the fixes in the audits.  
**Owner:** Barrels Grenada engineering (design mastery programme, issue #1453)  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-23

## How to read this

The owner asked for reference material to sit beside the audits. For each app below, the audit was read first (its main journeys and worst findings), then Mobbin was searched for screens that show the same journey done well. Every screen listed here was opened and looked at before it was described. A reference that was supplied by the owner or already named in an audit, and that was not opened again for this document, is marked "saved earlier".

Rules for using these:

- Borrow the idea, never the look. Every app keeps its own lane, colours and type (see the lane specs in `docs/design/`).
- Each entry says what to borrow and what not to. "Not" matters as much as "borrow": many references are from apps with a different audience.
- Where Mobbin had no good match, the section says so plainly.
- Links go to Mobbin and may need a Mobbin sign-in.

## Events

Journeys: event page and ticket chooser on a phone, checkout, my tickets, the door result, the organiser overview and editor. Worst audit findings: "admitted" and "refused" look almost alike at the door, and on a phone the ticket chooser starts below the screen under a button that does nothing yet.

- **DICE, event page with ticket chooser (phone).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/973cbdf3-7ad4-47cc-8ba0-4e817592f022). The flyer fills the top, then title, date and place, and a white card with the ticket type, a plus and minus counter and one big checkout button, all in the first screen. Borrow: the chooser and its button sitting together on the first screen, with the button label stating what it will do. Not: the full-bleed flyer that pushes everything else off, since long descriptions still need a place.
- **Open, class page with quantity and checkout (phone).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/bda54e7f-1a41-4692-9a4e-7097e08844ad). A dark page where the date, place and the person are short, the quantity counter sits in the middle and a single wide "Checkout" button is fixed at the bottom. Borrow: a short summary above a counter, with the main button pinned at the bottom of the screen. Not: the very low-contrast minus button, which is hard to see.
- **Eventbrite, Tickets list (phone).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/0d04ebee-04f4-44ce-95f5-9d388307461a). Tabs for Upcoming and Past, then ticket-shaped cards with the date and time, title, place, image and a small QR icon, and a "Coming up" heading below. Borrow: Upcoming and Past tabs and the ticket-stub card shape for my tickets. Not: the large photo on every card, which makes the list long.
- **Meetup, a single ticket (phone).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/427c632f-e3ad-471e-8652-1d95b622e4f3). One ticket on its own: a large QR code, a dotted tear line, then the event name, place, holder name and group, with a Download button. Borrow: the QR code as the whole screen, big enough to scan from arm's length, with the name under it for the person at the door. Not: the busy blurred background.
- **Luma, check-in screen for hosts (phone).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/6f2ca8bd-8c6a-4bf5-9ac7-7e2336d30ef9). A dark scanner with a green pill at the top that says "Guest checked in." with a tick, a List and Scan switch below. Borrow: a tick plus words in a clearly green shape for the good result, and a switch to the guest list. Not: the small pill; the audit asks for a result that fills the screen.
- **Posh, scanning a ticket for the wrong event (phone).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/681a9247-44ab-4844-a2d0-470bc48d5ca3). The refusal is written as large white words inside the scanner frame ("Order does not belong to this event") with a running count of scanned tickets at the top. Borrow: refusal in plain words, big, in the middle of the camera view, with the running count always visible. Not: white on black with no colour or icon; add the stop shape the audit asks for.
- **Eventbrite, event dashboard (desktop).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/fb27a4b1-dc47-4b7a-95c3-cf8c02539834). Left side steps (Build event page, Add tickets, Publish), then tickets sold as "0/25", page views, quick actions and sales by ticket type. Borrow: a checklist of set-up steps in the sidebar and "sold of total" as one number. Not: the "recommended actions" marketing prompt.
- **Posh, event overview (desktop).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/7eeeb253-5a1f-4ba4-b63c-7e1c9c9d7a8b). Three number tiles (tickets sold, page visits, conversions), a day, week, month range switch and one area chart. Borrow: a few big numbers first, then a single chart with a simple range switch. Not: conversion rate for an organiser with a handful of orders; it will mislead.
- **Eventbrite, purchasing a ticket (flow, saved earlier).** [Flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/da7d4575-6036-4c60-a292-d6fe3d5a5a38). Borrow: the order of steps for checkout. Not opened again for this document.
- **Meetup, scan a ticket (flow, saved earlier).** [Flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/287ca57a-2e55-40c7-b78f-18a5ef70225c). Borrow: how the host side moves from the camera to a result. Not opened again for this document.

## Weather site (the GMS public weather site)

Journeys: now and today at a glance, multi-day forecast, the units switch, the warning banner, the email sign-up form with errors, coming-soon pages. Worst audit findings: the units switch exists only on the home hero, and nothing on the site shows it was pressed.

- **Apple Weather, 10-day forecast (phone).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/c56345fa-fc98-445f-9a38-3c8b44297e28). One card with a row per day: day name, small icon, low, a thin bar showing where the day sits in the range, and high. Borrow: the range bar, which makes ten days readable at a glance without a chart. Not: the glass blur over a photo background; text contrast on the site must stay high.
- **The Weather Channel, home (phone).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/1c6acbe7-6b08-4c7c-ab73-4f6f1bf8a76e). Place name at the top, Today / This Week tabs, an hourly strip with temperature and chance of rain, then tiles for feels-like, humidity, wind, rain and UV with plain words ("High", "Good"). Borrow: tiles that pair a number with a word, and an hourly strip with the rain chance under each hour. Not: the many tiles at once; the site's four data cards are enough.
- **The Weather Channel, Severe Weather Alerts (phone).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/087493d2-1732-4ffe-a35c-af6c3464c230). A sheet with a warning icon, the type in capitals, the times, then "Action Recommended" before the long description, and a "View on Radar" button. Borrow: putting what to do before the detail. Not: the all-capitals legal wording of the description; use plain sentences.
- **Not Boring Weather, home with a warning pill (phone).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/8e62632f-0569-4f20-9ed1-dc340e164f8c). A dark rounded pill under the place name with a warning triangle and "Moderate rain warning +2", above a very large temperature. Borrow: a compact banner with an icon, the level in words and a "+2" count when more than one is in effect. Not: the dramatic 3D artwork; the site's warnings must stay calm and official.
- **Apple Weather, Units (phone).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/702623ec-8704-4d6d-a79c-0a3c4b6562a7). A list with Celsius, Fahrenheit and "Use system setting", a tick on the chosen row, other units below and "Restore Defaults". Borrow: all unit choices on one page with a visible tick, plus a restore button. Not: burying it in settings; the owner wants a switch in the masthead, so use this as the full page the masthead switch leads to.
- **Substack, sign-up with an error (desktop).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/82ce0541-ff23-4ba3-86d4-2c6263d0a93a). A single email field in a dialog, the error in red directly under the field with a "sign in to continue" link, and the main button below. Borrow: the error beside the field it belongs to, with the next step as a link inside the sentence. Not: the dialog over a busy page; the alerts sign-up is better as a plain form on its own page.
- **Apple Weather, flow (saved earlier).** [Flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/6ea9debf-2115-4bbc-83a3-b2a38682db57). Not opened again for this document.
- **Coming-soon and empty pages:** no good match found on Mobbin for a public service that says "not yet available" honestly. The audit says the site already does this well; nothing to add.

## Printix scanner

Journeys: pairing a device, scanning, success and refusal results, typed entry, offline, live count. Worst audit findings: the "yes" and "no" screens differ mostly by colour, and an iPhone Home Screen app cannot take a pairing code.

- **Posh, wrong-event refusal (phone).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/681a9247-44ab-4844-a2d0-470bc48d5ca3). Large plain words in the middle of the scanner with a count of scanned tickets at the top, a torch and camera-flip button below. Borrow: words big enough to read from arm's length, a running count always on screen, torch within thumb reach. Not: the lack of colour and icon.
- **Luma, host check-in (phone).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/6f2ca8bd-8c6a-4bf5-9ac7-7e2336d30ef9). Green pill with a tick and "Guest checked in.", a torch and settings button, and a List / Scan switch at the bottom. Borrow: the List and Scan switch, which is a natural place for the typed entry and the saved list. Not: the pill size, too small for a result read in half a second.
- **BeReal, "That's your own QR!" (phone).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/f62efa4d-f6a2-4ad2-8492-5fa4ac34394b). The camera stays visible while a bottom sheet explains the problem in two sentences with one "Got it" button. Borrow: a refusal that says why and what to try next, with one button. Not: a sheet that covers the lower third on a gate phone; the refusal should fill the screen.
- **Comet, Add device (phone).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/3670b77f-56c3-49f6-bd8c-4befe2a80b1f). A large QR code, one instruction paragraph, a "View sync code" link for typing instead and a Done button. Borrow: always offering the typed code beside the QR code. Not: the long instruction text; gate staff will not read it.
- **Brave Browser, Add a Tablet or Phone (phone).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/53c3fdf5-a622-4484-aa67-8ed340ca85c0). Tabs for QR Code and Code Words, a red line "Treat this code like a password", and a countdown that says how long the code is valid. Borrow: a visible countdown on the gate code, which matches the app's two-minute hide. Not: the second tab of words; one typed number is simpler.
- **Qantas, not connected (phone).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/18f77caf-19d5-4199-98b4-a36f34096d23). A centred icon, "You're not connected", one line of advice, a red "Try again" link, and a short message at the bottom. Borrow: a plain sentence and one retry action. Not: a full-page replacement; the scanner must stay usable offline, so a badge is better, as the app already has.
- **Klook, scan QR (flow, saved earlier).** [Flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/49013f4c-d34e-4354-b32f-6e59091e95f2). Not opened again for this document.

## Bookings

Worst audit findings: customers cannot reach the business, the day and time picker is a wall of chips, and the services are listed twice.

Category pills across the top, section headings, one card per service with name, time, price and a round plus button. Borrow: one list with category pills that jump to a section, and a plus button for each service. Not: the long clipped descriptions on cards.
- **Careem, Service details with add-ons (phone).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/6b740a44-3557-447d-af49-bd4140596f29). A progress line with "Next step: Popular add-ons", category pills, a photo, then services with price and a green "Add" button, with a running Total and Next button fixed at the bottom. - **IHG Hotels, Review your reservation (phone).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/c22fe527-84d9-4cb4-9f48-1e3f358f3355). A green tick line first ("Change or cancel for free anytime before 6:00 pm on Jun 14"), then the policy headed "Free Cancellation", check-in and out, and a fixed button showing the price. Borrow: the key rule as one tick line at the top, with the full policy below it. Not: the long legal paragraph; keep the policy to a few lines.
- **World of Hyatt, Confirmation (phone).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/4b86c1ea-0a7f-4a91-a156-4cdc39b8ba1d). A week strip at the top with the chosen day circled, an hour grid below, colour blocks sized by length, and a round add button at the bottom right. Borrow: the week strip above one day's hours, which suits a salon owner on a phone. Not: the thin all-day row and generic colours; use the app's own status colours.
- **Amie, calendar with a toast (phone).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/1a15f703-d684-4121-bfe5-26bb5d4d485f). Events as soft coloured blocks with the duration at the right edge and a toast "Event scheduled / Guest was notified". Borrow: the toast that says the customer was told. Not: the combined task list below the calendar.

## Sign-in

Already referenced in the audit: two-factor setup from Customer.io and Railway. New: sign-in with an emailed code, join, forgot password. Worst audit findings: the emailed-code step is a dead end if the code does not arrive, and text fields are 14 pixels so phones zoom.

- **Quo, Verify your identity (phone).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/1201764a-27c6-4e1e-9e62-a7d9c428850c). The email is shown in a field with an "Edit" link, one code field with a floating label, a Continue button, and "Didn't receive an email? Resend". Borrow: "Edit" beside the address (fixes a typo without starting over) and Resend directly under the code field. Not: nothing notable; this is close to what the audit asks for.
- **StubHub, Enter Verification Code (phone).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/3d32185f-1a49-48b1-a878-f31f34c52989). Six separate boxes for the digits, a Verify button that stays grey until the code is complete, then "Didn't receive the code? Resend Code" and "Still having trouble? Contact support". Borrow: two escape routes under the code field, resend first and a human second, which answers the dead end the audit names. - **Cal.com, Reset link sent (desktop).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/d1de78d7-ef28-48c9-8f02-6f5f05a39956). A small card: "Reset link sent", the address, "check the spam folder or reach out to support", and "Back to sign in". Borrow: telling people where to look next (spam folder, support) and a back link. Not: nothing; very close to ideal.
- **7shifts, Email Sent (desktop).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/47814c09-9184-4c91-8c93-b6c450efe5fb). The same step in fewer words with a plain "Back to login" link. Borrow: brevity as the floor. Not: it has no hint for a missing email; Cal.com is better there.
- **Loom, create account (desktop).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/389204de-eb10-45c0-a7ea-a609c63f582c). Email, name and password on one card, a strength bar with the unmet rules listed under it, a disabled Continue, and "Already have an account? Sign in" below. Borrow: the rules shown as a short list before the person fails, and the sign-in link under the form. Not: the disabled button with no reason given; the app's own convention is to say what is missing.
- **Faire, Welcome! Let's get started (desktop).** [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/9435af96-1fb6-46c7-9b67-3fd456ccc485). Two name fields and a password with a show/hide eye and "8 characters minimum", very little else. Borrow: the minimum written under the field, and a show/hide eye. Not: nothing; useful as the simplest version of Join, which the owner wants instant.

## Patterns worth borrowing across apps

- **A result is an icon, a word and a colour, never colour alone.** Luma, Posh and Klook all pair a tick or plain words with the result. Applies to the door, the gate and the status pages.
- **Put the next action where the thumb is.** DICE, Open and Careem keep the main button and a running total fixed at the bottom of the phone screen. Applies to the ticket chooser, the booking form and the account's recommended step.
- **Say what to do next beside the error.** Quo, Substack and Cal.com put the fix (Edit, Resend, sign-in link, check your spam folder) in the same place as the problem. Applies to every form and to the emailed-code step.
- **State rules and waiting times before they bite.** Loom lists password rules; Check says the code arrives within a minute; Brave shows a countdown on the pairing code. Applies to sign-in, join and the gate code.
- **Show the filter and the time zone.** Skillshare shows "All Categories"; Cloudflare writes the zone beside the time range; Snowflake shows a count. Applies to the portfolio, audit log and admin tables.
- **Status first, detail second.** Better Stack and OpenAI lead with one sentence of overall state; the Weather Channel alert puts "Action Recommended" before the long text. Applies to the weather warnings and the admin overview.
- **One list with jump pills beats tabs that hide nothing.** Fresha's category pills scroll to sections in one list. Applies to the Babydoll menu and the bookings services.
- **Make states a tab, and match the button to the state.** Eventbrite and Klook split Upcoming and Past, and Klook shows only the action that fits. Applies to my tickets and my bookings.
