# Grenada ICT law for feature development

**Status:** Active reference

**Owner:** Barrels Grenada engineering

**Last updated:** 2026-11-18

Use this guide when a feature changes what Grenmet collects, who may see it, what a user agrees to, who receives money, or who can issue an official product. It covers Grenadian laws relevant to the platform and its GAA/GMS work. A new country, regulated activity or client mandate needs its own scope review.

The engineering actions below are ways to implement or investigate the law. A numbered Act establishes enactment. Where a start date, amendment or sector approval affects a decision, record the operative provision and Gazette order in the feature record. The Ministry directs readers to the Government Printery for the authoritative text. [Laws of Grenada](https://www.laws.gov.gd/)

This guide is an engineering aid, not legal advice. Confirm with counsel before relying on a section, a start date or a deadline.

## Personal information and system access

| Instrument and scope | What to do in Grenmet |
| --- | --- |
| **Data Protection Act, No. 1 of 2023.** Section 4 ties scope to processing connected with commercial transactions and a territorial test. Section 1(2) allows different provisions to start by ministerial order. A commencement order was not verified in this review. [Government Acts index](https://www.laws.gov.gd/index.php/acts/search-result?filter_category_id=1344&id=1344&searchphrase=all&show_category=1&show_searchform=0&start=20) | For accounts, bookings, HR, offline ticket scans and AI inputs, record the purpose, processing condition, recipients, hosting location and expiry for each dataset. Identify who handles access and correction requests. Keep sensitive HR fields out of telemetry. Check each public-body workflow separately with GAA. Confirm the operative sections before promising a statutory deadline or a transfer mechanism. |
| **Electronic Crimes Act, No. 23 of 2013**, amended by No. 10 of 2014 and commenced 18 November 2016. Covers unauthorised access, interference and fraud. Read the amendment alongside the original offence list. [Act](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-No.-23-of-2013-Electronic-Crimes-Act.pdf) · [Amendment](https://grenadaparliament.gd/ova_doc/acts-2014/) · [Commencement](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/S.R.O.-59-of-2016-Electronic-Crimes-Act-Commencement-Order-2016.pdf) | Scope staff and device permissions by organisation. Test that one organiser cannot read or change another organiser’s tickets. Record who authorised support access and when it expired. |
| **Interception of Communication Act, No. 22 of 2013**, amended by No. 1 of 2014. Parliament lists commencement order SRO 29 of 2014. Relevant to capturing communications in transit and responding to lawful demands. [Act](https://grenadaparliament.gd/ova_doc/acts-2013/) · [Amendment](https://grenadaparliament.gd/ova_doc/acts-2014/) · [Order](https://grenadaparliament.gd/ova_doc/sros-2014/) | Use message IDs, timestamps and delivery results for routine debugging. Before enabling content capture, identify the authority, people permitted access and deletion date. Route interception requests to the designated legal contact and restrict the request record. |

For a financial investigation, the **Proceeds of Crime Act, No. 6 of 2012**, section 53, addresses production orders, including computer-held material. The **Budapest Convention on Cybercrime** entered into force for Grenada on 1 August 2024 and provides a framework for international cooperation. If a provider receives a preservation or disclosure request, record its authority, scope and expiry, preserve the specified records, and have the responsible legal contact validate any release. [2012 Act](https://grenadaparliament.gd/ova_doc/acts-2012/) · [Council of Europe treaty register](https://www.coe.int/en/web/conventions/full-list?module=signatures-by-treaty&treatynum=185)

## Health records

| Instrument and scope | What to do in Grenmet |
| --- | --- |
| **Data Protection Act, No. 1 of 2023, section 21.** Health data is sensitive personal data: process it with written consent or for medical purposes by a health professional or someone with an equivalent duty of confidentiality. Commencement is unverified (see above); Barrels Health builds to this standard regardless. **Health Practitioners Act, section 117:** keep practitioner records for 7 years after the last entry, then archive them. **Public Health Act, section 39:** doctors notify listed diseases without delay. | Follow ADR-0062 (`docs/adr/0062-health-records-foundation.md`): practice-owned charts, consented copies for sharing and insurer claims, a log of every chart view, archive instead of purge, and no real patient data before its go-live gate. |

## Digital forms, evidence and commerce

| Instrument and scope | What to do in Grenmet |
| --- | --- |
| **Electronic Transactions Act, No. 21 of 2013**, dated 3 October 2013. Sections 4, 6–15, 16–28 and 31–37 cover exclusions, records, contracts, signatures, government use, intermediaries, consumer information and unwanted communications. [Act](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-No.-21-of-2013-Electronic-Transactions.pdf) | For booking acceptance or HR approval, preserve the exact terms or form version, actor, action and time, and make the accepted record retrievable. Check section 4 and the receiving authority’s form requirements before replacing paper. For marketing, implement sender identification and opt-out handling. Keep emergency alert subscriptions distinguishable from promotions. |
| **Electronic Filing Act, No. 12 of 2013.** Applies to electronic filing with public authorities. [Act](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-No.-12-of-2013-Electronic-Filing-Act.pdf) | For a statutory submission, establish which authority accepts it, the prescribed format and when receipt counts. Retain the submitted version and the authority’s acknowledgement. Display internal draft, sent and acknowledged states distinctly. |
| **Electronic Evidence Act, No. 13 of 2013**, particularly sections 3–8 on authenticity, integrity and printouts. The **Evidence Act, Cap. 92**, sections 72–76, also addresses public documents and certified copies. [Electronic Evidence Act](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-No.-13-of-2013-Electronic-Evidence-Act.pdf) · [Evidence Act](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Cap92-EVIDENCE-ACT.pdf) | For an offline scan or corrected weather bulletin, retain the original event, device or issuer, event time, server receipt time and later corrections. Test whether a custodian can reproduce an export and explain its provenance. A timestamp alone cannot establish admissibility. |
| **Consumer Protection Act, No. 2 of 2018**, effective 1 July 2020. Relevant to representations about price, availability, terms and remedies. Read with section 36 of the Electronic Transactions Act. [Act](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-2-of-2018-Consumer-Protection-Act.pdf) · [Commencement](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/S.R.O.-44-of-2020-Consumer-Protection-Act-Commemcement-Order-2020.pdf) | Show the supplier, currency, total fees, cancellation terms and contact route before commitment. Test that the receipt matches the quoted total. Confirm the applicable remedy for a cancelled event before encoding a refund window. |
| **Electronic Transfer of Funds Crimes Act, No. 14 of 2013**, addresses misuse of electronic transfers. **Payment System and Services Act, No. 8 of 2025**, covers payment services and their regulation. [2013 Act](https://grenadaparliament.gd/ova_doc/acts-2013/) · [2025 Acts register](https://laws.gov.gd/index.php/acts/1374-2025) | Draw the money flow for deposits, ticket sales, refunds and organiser payouts. Identify the merchant, provider and holder of funds at every step. Before adding custody or settlement for others, establish with counsel and the ECCB whether licensing applies. For provider checkout, authenticate confirmations, reconcile transactions and test duplicate notifications. |

## Networks, publishing and official information

- **Electronic Communications Act, No. 4 of 2024**, **Telecommunications Act, No. 31 of 2000**, and **Telecommunications (Confidentiality in Networks and Services) Regulations, SRO 9 of 2016**. For a proposed radio link, spectrum use, or network service, ask Grenada’s NTRC which licensing regime and transitional provisions apply. Commencement of the 2024 Act was not verified here. Document whether Barrels operates the link or buys delivery from a carrier. [2024 Acts register](https://www.laws.gov.gd/index.php/acts/search-result?filter_category_id=1344&id=1344&searchphrase=all&show_category=1&show_searchform=0&start=20) · [2000 Act](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/11457) · [2016 regulations](https://www.ectel.int/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/GND-sro-9-2016-confidentiality-networks-services.pdf) · [NTRC](https://ntrc.gd/)
- **Copyright Act, No. 21 of 2011**, and **Trademarks Act, No. 1 of 2012**, amended by No. 2 of 2026. For source code, forecast graphics, map tiles, station feeds, CMS photos or logos, record the source, rights holder, permitted uses and attribution. Check redistribution and model-training rights separately. Keep the permission record with the asset before enabling syndication or downloads. [Copyright Act](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/Act-No.-21-of-2011-Copyright-Act.pdf) · [Trademarks Act](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/16395) · [2026 amendment notice](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/No.-12-of-2026-Wednesday-March-11th-2026-Extraordinary.pdf)
- **Civil Aviation Act, Cap. 54A**, and **2025 Civil Aviation Regulations** on meteorological services for air navigation (SRO 16), aeronautical information services (SRO 15) and air traffic services (SRO 13). For aviation weather, agree the issuing role, recipients, timeliness and correction procedure with GMS. Preserve the observation, product version, issue time and delivery history. Test that an expired or withdrawn bulletin cannot appear as the current one. Check the applicable aviation rules and approved operating procedure before changing a production feed. [Act](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Cap54A-CIVIL-AVIATION-ACT.pdf) · [Meteorological services](https://grenadaparliament.gd/ova_doc/s-r-o-16-of-2025-civil-aviation-meteorological-services-for-air-navigation-regulations/) · [Aeronautical information](https://grenadaparliament.gd/ova_doc/s-r-o-15-of-2025-civil-aviation-aeronautical-information-services-regulations/) · [Air traffic services](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/S.R.O.-13-of-2025-Civil-Aviation-Air-Traffic-Services-Regulations.pdf)
- **Disaster Management Act, No. 2 of 2023**. For a Common Alerting Protocol (CAP) warning, establish the authorised issuer and approval route for the hazard with the client. Exercise an update and cancellation across every public channel and retain the identifiers that connect them to the original alert. [Government Acts index](https://www.laws.gov.gd/index.php/acts/search-result?filter_category_id=1344&id=1344&searchphrase=all&show_category=1&show_searchform=0&start=20)
- **Digital Transformation Agency Act, No. 10 of 2026**. The 4 September Gazette lists the enacted law. When proposing shared identity, inter-agency data exchange or a government service integration, establish with the commissioning body which agency standards and approvals cover the interface. Record the specification and its owner before committing to an API contract. [Gazette notice](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/No.-41-of-2026-Friday-4th-September-2026.pdf) · [Government enterprise architecture portal](https://gea.gov.gd/)

## When a feature crosses a sector boundary

| Change | Additional law to check and decision to record |
| --- | --- |
| A new product name or cross-border launch needs trademark clearance | The **Corporate Affairs and Intellectual Property Office Act, Cap. 69A** identifies the national office. The **Madrid Protocol** took effect for Grenada on 15 March 2026 and provides an international registration route. Check the proposed name and markets before branding a public app. [CAIPO Act](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/text/483937) · [WIPO accession notice](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/treaties/notifications/details/treaty_madridp-gp_241) |
| Airport integrations expand to operational radio, navigation or rescue coordination | Determine GAA and ECCAA responsibilities under the **Airports Authority Act, Cap. 12** and **Eastern Caribbean Civil Aviation Authority Agreement Act, Cap. 85A**. Review the 2025 Civil Aviation Regulations for aeronautical search and rescue (SRO 17), radio navigational aids (SRO 33) or aeronautical radio frequency spectrum utilization (SRO 37), according to the equipment and service affected. [Acts directory](https://laws.gov.gd/index.php/map-of-folders/legislation-folder) · [Gazette regulations](https://grenadaparliament.gd/cat_doc/the-grenadian-government-gazette/page/5/) |
| Payroll exports add national insurance contributions | Use the **National Insurance Act, Cap. 205** and current contribution regulations to establish the applicable rate, dates and reporting format. Version rules by pay period. [NIS legislation](https://nisgrenada.org/download/national-insurance-act-cap205-compendium-2024/) |
| Flight displays expand to passenger manifests or booking records | **Advance Passenger Information and Passenger Name Record Act** and its 2026 commencement order. Confirm the authorised reporting party, fields, destination and retention period before ingesting identifiable passenger data. [Parliament’s SRO 20 of 2026 listing](https://grenadaparliament.gd/ova_doc/s-r-o-20-of-2026-advance-passenger-information-and-passenger-name-record-act-commencement-order-2026/) |
| Rosters or leave records become payroll inputs | **Employment Act, Cap. 89**. Confirm the employer’s recordkeeping duties and who may correct or inspect the underlying hours. [Employment Act](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Cap89-EMPLOYMENT-ACT.pdf) |
| Stores or approvals become a public purchasing system | **Public Procurement and Disposal of Public Property Act, No. 39 of 2014**, as amended, and **SRO 32 of 2015**. Confirm the entity and purchase are in scope. Preserve bids, approval history and the version of the award decision. [Act](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-No.-39-of-2014-Public-Procurement-and-Disposal-of-Public-Property-Bill.pdf) · [Regulations](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/S.R.O.-32-of-2015-Public-Procurement-And-Disposal-Of-Public-Property-Regulations.pdf) |
| Elections pages acquire individual voter records or publish results | **Representation of the People Act, Cap. 286A**. Confirm publication authority and permitted fields with the electoral authority. Preserve the official source and publication time for results and corrections. [Parliament’s Act collection](https://grenadaparliament.gd/ova_doc/volume-r/) |

## Applying the guide in a feature review

In the feature issue or design note, record the affected workflow, the law and provision, the party responsible, any unresolved legal question, and the acceptance check. For example, a booking deposit needs a named merchant, a quoted total and terms version, a verifiable payment confirmation, and a test that a repeated confirmation creates only one receipt. A new analytics provider needs a field inventory, processing purpose, recipient and deletion path. Put identifying examples and client-specific decisions in the approved private location.

Recheck the Acts, subsequent amendments and Gazette orders whenever the activity, recipient, jurisdiction or regulator guidance changes. Record who confirmed an open question and the effective date.
