## A week on—and beyond—the water

The Grenada Tourism Authority lists the fifth Dive & Conservation Festival for **26 October–1 November 2026**.

The programme opens in Carriacou and combines diving with activities focused on protecting the islands’ marine environment.

## You do not have to dive to take part

The published programme includes conservation discussions, birdwatching at Woburn Mangrove Reserve and a **Grand Anse beach clean-up on 31 October**.
