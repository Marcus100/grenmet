## Put it on your calendar

The **12°N, 61°W Grenada Film Festival** returns for its eighth season from **18–25 October 2026**, continuing its partnership with Movie Palace in Grand Anse.

The organiser says the programme represents 15 countries and territories, with narrative films, documentaries, animation and other forms of filmmaking.

## Start with Grenada Night

The opening on Sunday, 18 October focuses on Grenadian filmmakers and stories. This year’s theme, **ROOTS: What We Inherit**, explores family, culture, migration and community.

## Plan your visit

Use the [festival’s official website](https://www.1261filmfestival.com/) to choose screenings and check current tickets and programme details. This is an organiser-announced event preview; screening arrangements can change.
