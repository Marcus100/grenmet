## The dates

A Grenada and Carriacou Open Water Swim is announced for **19–22 November 2026**, with activities across both islands.

The tourism announcement identifies Jason Gooding of Odessey Timing and Events as the organiser and says the Grenada Tourism Authority is supporting the event.

## More than a race

The announced programme combines competitive swimming with destination experiences. Its inclusion of Carriacou gives readers a reason to look beyond the main island when following the event.

## What to confirm

The announcement is a starting point, not a complete participant guide. Confirm registration, distances, transport and the final schedule with the organiser before making arrangements.
