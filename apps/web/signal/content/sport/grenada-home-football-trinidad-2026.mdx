## The venue change

The Grenada Football Association announced that its October Concacaf Nations League home fixtures would move to **Hasely Crawford Stadium in Port of Spain**, citing poor field conditions at Kirani James Athletics Stadium.

The association said the move was intended to protect Grenada’s competition standing and avoid further sanctions.

## The next scheduled fixture

The GFA’s announcement lists **Grenada versus Bonaire on Monday, 5 October 2026 at 8pm**. Its other October doubleheader was scheduled for 2 October; this brief does not report that match’s result.
