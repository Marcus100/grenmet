## What Grenada said

Grenada’s delegation raised concern about hardship in Cuba at the United Nations General Assembly, according to CMC reporting published by NOW Grenada on 29 September.

Delegation chair Ingrid Jackson highlighted the situation facing ordinary households, including children, older people and other vulnerable groups.

## Why it belongs here

This is Grenada speaking on a regional issue in an international forum. It offers readers a view of the country’s diplomatic priorities beyond domestic politics.
