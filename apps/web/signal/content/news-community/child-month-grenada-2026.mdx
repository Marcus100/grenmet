## The month ahead

Grenada’s Ministry of Education and Child Month Committee have announced activities for **1–31 October 2026**, centred on children’s health, safety and shared responsibility.

The ministry’s 29 September announcement lists street marches during **12–16 October**, Child Sabbath and Child Sunday on **17–18 October**, and a blanket evening/bonfire at Pearls Playing Field on **22 October**.

## Beyond the events

The stated focus includes nutrition, technology use, parental involvement and protecting children. Schools, churches and daycare centres are encouraged to organise activities with children and parents.

## Before heading out

The national list does not provide every school’s arrangements or each event’s starting time. Check with the relevant organiser for local details and changes.
