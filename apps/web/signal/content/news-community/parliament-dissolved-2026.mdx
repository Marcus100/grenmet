## What happened

The Office of the Houses of Parliament announced that the fourth session of Grenada’s eleventh Parliament was dissolved with effect from **2 October 2026**. The notice cites a proclamation issued that day by Governor-General Dame Cécile La Grenade and is signed by Clerk of Parliament Finley Jeffrey.

## What to watch

The notice establishes the dissolution date. It does **not** announce a polling date. Readers should look for the separate official election announcement before making plans around a voting day.
