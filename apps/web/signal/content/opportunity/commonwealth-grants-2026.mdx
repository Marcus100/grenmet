## What is available

The Commonwealth Foundation’s current annual call lists Grenada among eligible countries. It offers grants for individual not-for-profit organisations and a separate stream for collective action.

## Who should look

The call supports advocacy around climate and environmental justice, economic inclusion and freedom of expression. Being based in Grenada alone does not establish eligibility: registration, experience, income and project requirements also apply. Use the Foundation’s eligibility quiz and guidance before preparing an application.

## Watch the closing time

The page gives **26 October 2026** as the closing date but contains conflicting times: 1pm GMT near the top and noon GMT in its submission instructions. Confirm the time with the Foundation and submit well ahead of the deadline. This summary does not resolve that discrepancy.
