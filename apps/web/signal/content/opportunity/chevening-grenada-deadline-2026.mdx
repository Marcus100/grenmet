## The deadline

Chevening’s Grenada page says applications for **2027–28 scholarships** close on **6 October 2026 at 11:00 UTC**. That is **7am in Grenada**, not the end of the working day.

## Before submitting

Start from the official Grenada page linked below. Follow its application guidance and eligibility requirements, check your information and submit before the closing time.

The [official application timeline](https://www.chevening.org/scholarships/application-timeline/) says the online application system will no longer be accessible after the deadline.

## Keep this distinction clear

This is an application deadline, not a promise of an award. Requirements and selection decisions belong to Chevening; check the official guidance rather than relying on a forwarded summary.
