# Elections Grenada photography

Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons on 3 October 2026. Archive photographs that
illustrate places; they do not document the events in the stories they appear
beside. Local WebP files are resized (1400px or 1200px wide), compressed and
stripped of metadata. No source image is hotlinked. Captions on the site carry
the credit and licence; `src/data/photos.ts` holds the same details.
