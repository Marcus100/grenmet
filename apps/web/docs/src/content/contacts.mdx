# Staff directory and duty roster

## Find a colleague
Open **Staff** in the portal for the staff directory. Use the information visible to your account. Report incorrect details through the established administrative process.

## Check your duty roster
Open **Duty Roster** and check the displayed period and assignment. Confirm any discrepancies with the person responsible for the roster before acting on them. A requested change is not an approved change.

## Requests and approvals
Open **Human Resources → Forms** for available request forms and **Approvals** for work assigned to you. Access and actions depend on your role and the request state. Leave, shift-swap, timesheet and import workflows are being checked individually for launch.

## Documents
Use [documents and forms](/attachments) for the distinction between a reusable form and your submitted record.

## Hurricane contacts
The plan's [emergency personnel appendix](/appendix/emergency-personnel) remains available separately from the staff directory. Check the context and currency of contact information before using it.
