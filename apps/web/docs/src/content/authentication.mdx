# Signing in and account security

## Sign in
Open the staff portal and follow its sign-in link to the shared authentication service. Use the account assigned to you. Successful sign-in establishes your identity; it does not grant every staff permission.

## Forgotten password
The sign-in service includes a **Forgot password** page and a reset-password flow. Follow the instructions in the reset email. If it does not arrive, check your spam folder and contact your administrator; email delivery must be working in the selected environment.

## Authenticator protection
The account security page provides authenticator setup. Add the displayed setup key to your authenticator app and confirm with its current six-digit code. Keep the setup key private. An enabled authenticator is required on subsequent sign-ins according to the account's settings.

Do not include passwords, setup keys, recovery codes or session tokens in support reports.

## Access denied
If you can sign in but cannot perform an action, ask the administrator to review your role. Do not use another person's account. See [roles and permissions](/groups).

## Shared workstations
Sign out at the end of your session. Report suspected account misuse promptly through your established support contact.
