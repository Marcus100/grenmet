# Appendix A: List of Emergency Personnel

The 2024 plan lists the people called on during cyclone operations, with their phone numbers and home areas. Those personal details are not published on this website. Staff get the full list from the Grenada Meteorological Service and the Grenada Airports Authority duty office. {{ className: 'lead' }}

## Who is on the list

| Group | Roles |
| --- | --- |
| Meteorologists and forecasters | Duty meteorologists and forecasters |
| Duty managers | Airport duty managers |
| Management | Chief Executive Officer, Human Resource Officer, Chief Fire and Rescue officer |
| Maintenance | Air conditioning, electrical, plumbing, buildings, fields and grounds |
| Stores | Stores officer |
| Aeronautical Information Services | AIS officer |
| Carriacou | Carriacou airport contact |
| Security | Security supervisors |

## Keeping the list current

The duty office reviews the list before each hurricane season and after staff changes. Ask the duty office for the current copy rather than relying on an old printout.
