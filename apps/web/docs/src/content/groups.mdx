# Roles and permissions

## Your account and role
Sign-in identifies you. Your assigned roles and permissions determine the records and actions available to you. Seeing a navigation item does not grant permission to read or change its data.

## Preparation and approval
Preparing a forecast, reviewing a request and publishing an alert are different responsibilities. Follow the organisation's review process. Do not share an approver's account to complete a workflow.

## Request access
Tell the administrator which task you need to perform and which page or action is denied. The administrator can review the account and its assignments. Role-management changes are part of the launch verification work.

## Access denied
If the interface reports a permission problem, keep the page and action details and follow [troubleshooting](/errors). Signing in again will not grant a role that your account does not have.

## Public documentation
This documentation hub is public guidance. Its availability does not make staff records public or grant access to protected administration functions.
