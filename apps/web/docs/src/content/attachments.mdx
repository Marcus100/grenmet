# Documents and forms

## Staff documents
Open **Human Resources → Documents** in the staff portal. The documents shown depend on the implemented workflow and your account's permissions. Keep personnel records in the authorised staff system.

## Forms and submitted records
Open **Human Resources → Forms** to find the available forms. Read the requested fields before submitting. A form template is not evidence of an approved request; check the submitted record and its workflow state.

## Uploads and downloads
Follow the file-type and size instructions displayed by the relevant form. Confirm the result after saving or uploading. If a download fails, check your session and permissions before reporting it. Do not assume every form supports attachments.

## Operational references
The hurricane plan remains organised by [pre-season actions](/pre-season/general), [warning-issued actions](/warning-issued/met-department), [during-storm actions](/during-hurricane-storm) and [recovery](/after-hurricane-storm).

The [emergency personnel](/appendix/emergency-personnel) and [MBIA vehicles](/appendix/mbia-vehicles) appendices remain at their existing addresses.

## Corrections and support
Report an incorrect form or document to its responsible owner. Include the title and version or date if available. See [getting help](/errors).
