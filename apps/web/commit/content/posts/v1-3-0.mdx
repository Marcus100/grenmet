Version 1.3.0 carries the work merged after v1.2.0. Its database changes only add
columns or relax a rule, so the previous version still runs against the new
schema. Several features arrive switched off: they do nothing until their keys or
settings are added, which happens on staging first.

### Signing in

- **A Barrels sign-in:** the shared sign-in page now has the Barrels look instead
  of the weather service's, and no longer lists other organisations' apps to
  people who are not signed in.
- **Code first:** the page opens with "Email me a code", with "Use password
  instead" right below. The code step can send a new code after a minute or
  start again with a different email, and screen readers hear when a new code is
  sent.
- **Easier on phones:** fields no longer make the phone zoom in, and buttons are
  larger to tap.
- **Spam protection, ready:** the emailed-code step can check for bots with
  Cloudflare Turnstile. It stays off until its keys are added.
- **Shorter setup for staff:** someone who has just signed in with an emailed
  code can set up an authenticator without a second emailed code, and stays
  signed in afterwards instead of signing out and in again.

### Accounts and access

- **The last superuser stays:** the last active superuser can no longer be
  deactivated, deleted or offboarded, and HR staff cannot offboard a superuser.
- **Removing a superuser signs them out:** every session of the removed account
  ends at once.
- **Access follows the person who gave it:** removing or deleting an
  organisation admin ends the access they gave others, and access given by an
  admin who is suspended pauses until they are reinstated. An admin demoted a
  moment earlier can no longer still change someone else's access.

### Bookings and Babydoll

- **Contact options per business:** each business can add its phone, WhatsApp,
  Instagram and email in Studio settings, and the booking page shows the ones it
  filled in. Messages that ask customers to contact the business now show how.
- **Design polish:** larger tap areas, clearer press and focus states, and error
  messages that clear as soon as a field is fixed.
- **Payments, in test mode:** a business can choose to take the full price at
  booking, paid on Stripe's own page, once Stripe test keys are set. Without
  them, nothing changes. Real payments come later.

### Events

- **No category is fine:** organisers can clear an event's category. Uncategorised
  events still appear in all events and search.

### Tickets

- **Gate scanner screens:** paired phones scan tickets and show a full green
  screen for admitted and a full red screen with the reason for refused, with a
  live count of people in. The scanner enforces a strict security policy and will
  live at scan.barrels.gd.

### Sites

- **A new barrels.gd:** the company site now lists twelve services, from web and
  software to digital marketing, data analysis, training and weather data, with
  Barrels products and examples of client work. It moved to Cloudflare.
- **Easier to find:** every public site now has a sitemap, a robots file, link
  previews and structured data for search engines, while account, ticket,
  booking-management and staff pages stay out of search.
- **Elections on Cloudflare:** the Elections site builds as a static site, and
  today's date is worked out in the visitor's browser so it never goes stale.

### Weather

- **2027 tides:** the tide table loader can now check a file before loading it,
  so the 2027 Prickly Bay table can be loaded safely before the current one runs
  out.
- **Daily newsletter, ready:** the daily climate email can be switched on per
  environment, and its links and posts now point at the weather site.
- **Ready for storms:** public alert and satellite feeds can be served from
  Cloudflare for up to a minute when many people check at once. Web pages and
  anything signed-in are never cached.
