Version 1.0.6 carries 36 pull requests since v1.0.5 and one additive database
migration (the almanac tables), so the previous version still runs against the new
schema. Everything in it ran on staging, including a signed-in check of the GMS staff
portal, before it reached production.

### Weather service

- **No old data shown as current:** an observation more than two hours old says when
  it was observed and that it is older than usual. A forecast past its valid-to time
  keeps its words and says when it expired and when the next issue is due. Nothing
  is hidden.
- **Menu that matches what GMS issues:** green-level products are GMS alerts
  everywhere; pages GMS does not publish yet stay in the menu with a Coming label,
  read out as "coming soon" by screen readers. Moved pages redirect, the CAP feeds
  page lists the hub's XML, JSON, RSS and map feeds, and the issued archive covers
  every product kind.
- **From the Desk:** three cards across on tablets and four on laptops, with no
  scrollbar. Temperatures show °C or °F, and feels-like follows the US National
  Weather Service heat index, checked against MetPy.
- **Tides:** a predicted tides page for Prickly Bay, marked "not for navigation".

### GMS products desk

- **One form layout:** a list of problems at the top that links to each field,
  messages next to each field, and an action bar at the bottom with the status and
  Save, Preview and Publish. Publish stays off while problems remain.
- **Forecast and marine fields:** weather is a multiple choice mapped to the WMO
  present-weather codes, marine wind is in knots, and marine bulletins are numbered
  per event.
- **Fill from data:** sun, moon and tide fields fill from a yearly almanac computed
  with Astral and the predicted tide table. A "Fill empty fields from NHC" button
  fills marine wind and sea from the NHC Offshore Waters forecast for the Windward
  Islands. It never overwrites a typed value, refuses guidance older than 12 hours,
  and leaves the wind empty when NHC gives different values for two areas.

### eRegister

- **WMO formats at acceptance:** each accepted SYNOP is encoded as BUFR with ecCodes
  tooling and checked by decoding it again, and as an IWXXM 2025-2 METAR. A failed
  BUFR check stops the send.
- **Reviewer override:** a reviewer, never the observer, can allow a failed BUFR
  check to be sent, with a written reason. The override covers only that exact
  message and is recorded in the audit history.
- **Save and approve:** observers save and approve an hour in one step, and an
  observation is emailed only once it is accepted.

### Platform

- **Explicit status rules:** CAP, eRegister, events, HR, payments, payroll,
  subscriptions, invoices, organisation verification, stores, forecasts and
  tropical systems each declare their allowed status changes in one table. An
  unexpected stored status now gives a clear refusal instead of a server error.
- SURFACE is locked to its own host, production monitors are ready for Better Stack,
  and deployed images are scanned daily.
