Version 1.9.0 carries the work merged after v1.8.0, most of it in Bookings ahead
of a salon going live on the platform this week. It has four database changes,
and each one only adds: new columns with safe defaults and two new tables. The
previous release still runs against the new schema, so a rollback stays simple.

### Bookings

- **Deposit terms a customer can read:** a business can mark its deposit as
  non-refundable once it has confirmed a booking. It can also set the lowest
  service price that takes its deposit rule. Each booking keeps the terms it was
  made on, so changing the rule later never rewrites an existing booking.
- **One plain rules line:** beside the Book button, the customer sees one
  sentence such as "EC$40 deposit, not refundable. Change or cancel up to 24
  hours before.", with the full policy one tap away. The manage page shows the
  same line from the booking's own terms.
- **Add-ons:** a business can offer extra services that are booked with a main
  service. They are priced and timed together on the booking form, the
  confirmation, the manage page, the emails and in Studio. Add-ons stay off until
  a business switches them on.
- **The right amount up front:** the amount due at booking now includes any
  add-ons. When add-ons push a booking past the deposit threshold, the emailed
  code step comes first.
- **Services by category:** the public booking page groups services into
  categories the customer can open one at a time.
- **A private calendar feed:** a business can subscribe to its bookings from
  Google or Apple Calendar, and can rotate or turn off the link in Studio.
- **Names and privacy:** the manage-booking page now names the business the
  customer should contact. The secret in a manage link no longer reaches our
  rate-limit records. Calendar event titles also hide anything that looks like
  a phone number or email address typed into the name field.

### Weather alerts

- **The issued colour, as issued:** the public alert hub now shows each warning
  in the colour it was issued at. An orange warning reads "Be prepared", and
  the full red band is kept for the most severe level only.
- **Forms that wait for the page:** forms on the weather and Signal sites stay
  disabled until the page is ready. This means form details can no longer end
  up in the page address.

### Elections and HR

- **2026 provisional results** are loaded, and the results pages gain a new
  "declared" phase.
- **Read-only staff records:** an organisation's HR administrators can now open
  a staff member's record without being able to change it.

### Behind the scenes

We also wrote design audits for each app, with reference screens to compare
