Version 1.0.7 is two pull requests on top of v1.0.6, plus a CI fix. It has no
database migration, and the API changes are additive, so the previous version
still runs against it.

### Weather service

- **The evening, read correctly:** after the 18:00 forecast, today's tab says
  "Tonight" with tonight's low instead of a dash, and the link under the current
  temperature reads "Tonight: …, low …". Today's highest and lowest readings stay
  beside the temperature between the main observing hours: the API carries them
  forward from the last reading of the same Grenada day that has them.
- **A quieter forecast row:** the chance of rain moved out of the day row and into
  each day's details, so no word floats under one day.
- **Alert banner on phones:** one line with the level and "View alerts". When the
  alerts feed cannot be read, the advice to listen to local radio or call NaDMA
  still shows on phones.
- **Header status in words:** where the header has room, the alert status reads
  "No active alerts" (or the level) next to its icon, so it never relies on colour
  and an icon alone. Phones keep the icon.
- **Pictures:** the site resizes CMS pictures to the size they are shown at and
  keeps the copies, and the CMS now tells browsers to keep its media for a day. A
  thumbnail that used to download a 2 MB original now downloads a small copy.
- **No empty picture boxes:** official forecast, outlook, marine and climate cards,
  the Plan your week card and the explainer card show a credited photo of Grenada
  or regional weather when no desk picture is linked. A pinned desk post's picture
  still wins.
- **Smaller fixes:** Discover cards keep to four lines and name a fact's source
  once; news picture credits stay on one line; Plan your week says a quiet week in
  one sentence instead of six "None" labels; official and desk cards share one
  border, with the navy label marking what is official; the footer's social
  buttons are one round row.
- **Readable times:** issued product pages and the synopsis show Grenada wall-clock
  times such as "10 Nov 2026, 7:00 a.m." instead of revision stamps, and page
  titles match the homepage.

### GMS products desk

- **Every day lines up:** each outlook day in the evening forecast now has the same
  optional Conditions and Chance of rain fields as the first period. The website
  draws each day's icon from the forecaster's Conditions choice, with rain in the
  words or weather types taking priority, instead of guessing from the text.
- **Plain error messages:** a value out of range names its form field, for example
  "Waves from (m): must be between …". The CAP editor and the product preview say
  what went wrong in words instead of showing a raw HTTP error, and a failed CAP
  save is reported so it can be fixed.

### Platform

- **Cookie choice:** on every site that asks, "Accept cookies" and "Essential
  cookies only" are now equal buttons, with settings as a link.
- **CI:** a push to `dev` no longer cancels an earlier `dev` run, so every merge is
  tested.
