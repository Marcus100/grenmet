Version 1.8.0 carries the work merged after v1.7.0. It has six database
changes, and each one only adds: new tables, new columns that are optional or
have defaults, and one seed row. The previous release still runs against the
new schema.

### Bookings

- **Plan and billing:** Studio has a Plan and billing page. It shows the
  current subscription in plain words, the monthly price, and a button to
  start or continue checkout. Billing runs in test mode only; until it is
  switched on, the page says "Billing is not switched on yet".
- **App subscriptions:** subscriptions follow clear states, with a signed
  webhook, replay protection and a safe retry window. Free trials are set per
  business.
- **Business-wide deposits:** a business can ask for a deposit as a percentage
  or a fixed amount that counts toward the price. It is off by default and
  applies only when a payment route exists. Studio Settings shows a live
  preview, the booking page says when the deposit is refunded, and the manage
  page shows what was paid and what remains.
- **Finding businesses:** the Bookings home page can list businesses that opt
  in. Studio staff actions are audited, My bookings has tabs, and every public
  business page has a "Report this listing" link.

### Weather warning desk

- **Audit history** on each alert, and a Raise to warning button on a plain
  alert.
- **One raise at a time:** a published warning blocks a second raise on the
  server.
- **Automatic expiry:** a published warning now expires once every end time
  has passed, and is recorded as expired by the system.
- **Easier to tap:** desk menu links are at least 44 px tall, and the audit
  history shows readable labels instead of raw ids.

### The HR app

- **A new app:** it starts with sign-in, an organisation picker, a staff list
  for organisation administrators, and My record, where each person sees their
  own employment details and leave balance. It was not yet deployed in this
  release.

### Platform

- **Cookieless funnel analytics** for Bookings, Events and Account: no cookies,
  no storage, only allow-listed ids, and only on production hosts. In this release
  it was off until a key was set.
- **Events** shows active tickets next to issued tickets, and the admin console
  asks before revoking a key or deleting a feature flag.
- **Patched images:** container images were moved to newer patch releases
  after a staging scan found them one patch behind.
