It also hardens uploads, refunds and logs, and adds a health check for uptime monitoring.
date: 2026-11-25

Version 1.10.0 carries the work merged after v1.9.0. Most of it is in Bookings.
It has two database changes. Each one only adds columns with safe defaults, so
the previous release still runs against the new schema.

### Finding a business

- **A new home page:** each business has a card in its own colour, with its
  logo or cover photo and up to three of its services with prices.
- **Browse by type:** tiles for beauty, hair, health, fitness, lessons, tours,
  events and more, so people can find a kind of business, not only a name.
- **Cover photos:** a business can upload one in Studio. Like logos, the image
  is cleaned of camera and location data when it is uploaded.

### Booking on a phone

- **Step by step:** choose a service, then a time, then enter your details,
  with a short summary always in view.
- **Straight to a service:** a business's own website can link to one service,
  so customers land on the right step.
- **A tidier time step:** dates page in one compact row.
- **Clear limits:** a customer can hold a few upcoming bookings with one
  business online. Past that, the form explains how to reach the business.

### Running the day in Studio

- **A new layout:** Studio looks like the staff portal we use elsewhere, and it
  can be installed on a phone's home screen.
- **Needs confirming:** requests waiting for the business sit at the top, each
  showing when it expires, with Call and WhatsApp buttons.
- **Requests expire on their own:** a request the business does not confirm in
  time cancels itself, frees the slot and tells the customer. Each business
  chooses the window, from an hour to a week, and the per-customer limit.
- **Past bookings close themselves:** a booking that ended two days ago is
  marked completed, so the agenda stays clean.

### Safety and reliability

- **Uploads:** a finished upload is copied to a location only the server
  writes to, so the original upload link can no longer replace it.
- **Refunds:** a deposit refund that is retried reuses the same key at the
  payment provider, so a customer is never refunded twice.
- **Logs:** links that carry a booking's private code no longer appear in the
  server's access log.
- **Sender names:** emails sent for a business use its name unless the name
  imitates Barrels, a government body or a bank. The check now works word by
  word, so ordinary names are no longer caught by mistake.
- **A health check:** a new endpoint lets an uptime monitor check that data
  feeds are fresh and emails are going out, without signing in.
- **Unused addresses:** addresses we have reserved but not yet used now send
  visitors to the main site instead of showing an error.

### Tests

A browser test now books, opens the manage link and cancels, both at phone and
laptop width, on every change to the booking pages.
