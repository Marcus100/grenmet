title: Refund terms for every event, one look for our emails, and clearer units on the weather site
summary: Version 1.6.0 lets each event set its own refund policy and cancellation fee, sends refund receipts and ticket reminders, adds manage links to booking emails, moves our emails onto one shared layout, and shows units on the forecast.
date: 2026-11-21
release: 1.6.0
---

Version 1.6.0 carries the work merged after v1.5.0. It has one database change,
and it only adds: five new columns for the refund policy (four on event listings
and one on orders), each with a safe default or left empty, plus checks on their
values. Events and Bookings got most of the new features.

### Events

- **Refund policy per event:** an organiser can choose whether an event is
  refundable, how many hours before the start refunds stop, and an optional
  cancellation fee. The terms show in the organiser editor, in the cancel flows,
  and as a one-line summary on the event page and ticket claim for paid tickets.
  Once an order has been paid, terms can only be made more generous, and a
  cancelled event always refunds everyone in full with no fee.
- **Receipts and reminders:** a refund now sends a receipt that says how much was
  refunded, whether it covers the whole order, any cancellation fee kept, and
  when the organiser cancelled the event, that it was cancelled. Ticket holders
  get one reminder email once their event is within 24 hours.
- **A working organiser console:** every menu item now opens the selected event's
  tickets, orders, attendees, door and finance pages, the console remembers the
  last event you opened, and the account block shows the signed-in person. The
  overview shows tickets issued, admitted and cash at the till (online payments
  are not totalled yet). Screens show EC$ and US$ labels, and a team member can be
  invited from the Team page.
- **Organiser profile edit (API):** an approved organiser can now change the
  profile name and bio through the API. The Settings screen that uses it follows.
- **WhatsApp drafts (pilot):** an opt-in pilot builds a WhatsApp announcement
  draft from the saved event facts. The organiser reviews the text and copies it
  to send by hand; nothing is sent and no draft is stored.

### Bookings

- **A manage link in the key emails:** the confirmation, deposit, reminder and
  reschedule emails carry a signed link to manage the booking, which expires 7
  days after the booking ends. Guests are also invited to create an account in
  the booking received, confirmed and reminder emails and on the manage page.
- **Jobs we can watch:** the booking reminder and unpaid-booking expiry jobs
  report to monitoring.

### One look for our emails

- **A shared layout:** booking and event emails, and the password reset, welcome,
  invitation and subscription emails, now use one layout: the business or
  product name, a clear title, the key details in a table and one large button.
  Every link is checked before it is shown, and booking and event emails also
  have a plain-text version. Staff notification emails changed look too.
- **Rendered when sent:** booking and event emails are stored as data and
  rendered at the moment they are delivered, with a fallback if the renderer is
  down, so the message still goes out. The account and invitation emails use the
  same look when the renderer is up; the fallback keeps their old look for now.

### Weather and sites

- **Units on the forecast:** the forecast day card and the day strip now show the
  unit next to the main temperatures (lows stay as a bare degree).
- **Report PDFs:** when a document preview fails, the message now depends on the
  cause (server error, expired session, or a reply in the wrong format) instead
  of always blaming the connection.
- **Our own site:** the Barrels homepage lists a wider range of ICT services.

### Safety and reliability

- **Security-policy fixes:** fixes for the four violations that the report-only
  content security policy reported in production (analytics hosts, sign-in links
  in Events and Auth, and a script probe on the MBIA contact page). Nothing is
  enforced yet; we confirm in the next production window.
- **Account deletion and rosters:** creating a membership and deleting an account
  now take the same lock, so they cannot overlap.
- **Safer database changes:** destructive migrations now have to be split into an
  additive step and a later removal, and CI enforces it.

### Tests, CI and docs

- **Accessibility checks:** the home, alerts and about pages of the public weather
  site are checked with axe against WCAG 2.1 A and AA in the browser journeys that
  run on every pull request.
- **Decision records:** records for the Shop Keeper and Salesbus commerce core and
  for one registry for every ticket, health insurers added to the health-records
  record, and a health records section in the guidance on Grenada's information
  and communications technology law.
- **Environment guide:** the environment templates and guide now cover every
  setting.
