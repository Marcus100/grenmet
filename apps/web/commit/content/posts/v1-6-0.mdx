title: Refunds that follow each event's own terms, one look for our emails, and clearer units on the weather site
summary: Version 1.6.0 lets each event set its own refund policy and cancellation fee, sends refund receipts and day-before ticket reminders, puts a manage link in every booking email, moves our emails onto one shared layout, and shows units on the weather forecast.
date: 2026-11-21
release: 1.6.0
---

Version 1.6.0 carries the work merged after v1.5.0. It has one database change,
which only adds four columns with safe defaults, so the previous version still
runs against the new schema. Events and Bookings got most of the new features.

### Events

- **Refund policy per event:** an organiser can choose whether an event is
  refundable, how many hours before the start refunds stop, and an optional
  cancellation fee. The terms show in the organiser editor, in the cancel flows
  and on the buyer's page, so people see them before they pay.
- **Receipts and reminders:** a refund now sends a receipt that says what was
  refunded and why, and ticket holders get a reminder about a day before the
  event.
- **A working organiser dashboard:** the event dashboard now shows real figures
  instead of placeholders.
- **Edit your organiser profile:** an approved organiser can change the profile
  name and bio.
- **WhatsApp drafts (pilot):** the organiser editor can prepare a WhatsApp
  announcement for the organiser to review and send. Nothing is sent for them.

### Bookings

- **A manage link in every email:** every customer email about a booking carries
  a link to change or cancel it, and guests are invited to create an account
  afterwards.
- **Jobs we can watch:** the booking reminder job reports to monitoring.
- **Clearer payment wording** on the booking, manage and studio pages (carried
  over from the last release, with display fixes).

### One look for our emails

- **A shared layout:** booking and event emails, and the password reset, welcome,
  invitation and subscription emails, now use one layout: the business or
  product name, a clear title, the key details in a table and one large button.
  Every link is checked before it is shown, and each email also has a plain-text
  version.
- **Rendered when sent:** an email is stored as data and rendered at the moment
  it is delivered, with a fallback if the renderer is down, so a rendering
  problem cannot lose a message.

### Weather and sites

- **Units on the forecast:** the forecast card and the day strip now show the
  unit next to each temperature.
- **Report PDFs:** the document preview now says why a preview is not available.
- **Our own site:** the Barrels homepage lists a wider range of ICT services.

### Safety and reliability

- **Fewer security-policy violations:** violations that the report-only content
  security policy found in production are cleared.
- **Account deletion and rosters:** creating a membership and deleting an account
  now take the same lock, so they cannot overlap.
- **Safer database changes:** destructive migrations now have to be split into an
  additive step and a later removal, and CI enforces it.
- **More monitors, same free plan:** the CMS, Events and Bookings now have
  uptime monitors.

### Tests, CI and docs

- **Accessibility checks:** the public weather pages are checked with axe against
  WCAG 2.1 A and AA on every pull request.
- **Decision records:** records for the health-records foundation, the Shop
  Keeper and Salesbus commerce core, and one registry for every ticket, plus
  guidance on Grenada's information and communications technology law.
- **Environment guide:** the environment templates and guide now cover every
  setting.
