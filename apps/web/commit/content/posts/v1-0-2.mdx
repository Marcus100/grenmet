Version 1.0.2 shipped the day after v1.0.1 and fixes what showed up once the new
homepage met real phones.

### Homepage

- No sideways scrolling on phones, and no scroll bar under From the Desk.
- The header menu clears the logo.
- No empty rain row in the day tabs, and the first tab says **Last** for an old
  reading.
- a.m. and p.m. times use the normal font, phone headings are shorter and Plan your
  week says "none" once.
- The Official product card is gone.

### Cookies

- Cookie settings moved from a floating button into the footer of every public app.
