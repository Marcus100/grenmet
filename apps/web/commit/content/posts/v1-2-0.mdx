Version 1.2.0 carries 24 pull requests since v1.1.0. It adds two database tables,
one for superuser invitations and one set for the ticket registry, and removes
nothing, so the previous version still runs against the new schema. It is a minor
release because people will notice it: Babydoll's customers book on a new page,
and a new way of granting superuser status replaces the old one.

### Bookings

- **The salon's Book buttons open its own booking page:** every Book button on the
  Babydoll site, and the old `/book` address, now open the studio's page on
  `bookings.barrels.gd` instead of the previous booking service. The owner booked
  through it on production first.
- **Signed-in customers type less:** a signed-in customer's name, email and phone
  are filled in, and a past customer is offered Book again.
- **An account after booking, if wanted:** a guest who books is invited to create
  a free account afterwards. Booking never waits on it.
- **Staff can move a booking:** staff change a booking's time in the studio and the
  customer is told. Each move sends its own notice, so two moves to the same time
  are not mistaken for one.
- **Nothing waits unseen:** a booking still waiting for staff to confirm it sends a
  reminder.
- **Fewer dead ends:** `/studio/<business>` opens the agenda, and an expired manage
  link explains why instead of showing a blank page.
- **Small accessibility fixes** on the booking page and the Babydoll site: larger
  touch targets, keyboard focus that shows, and page landmarks for screen readers.

### Accounts and superusers

- **Four ways in, one account:** a decision record sets out how each kind of person
  joins. Customers sign up with an email code and verify anything else later.
  Business members are invited. Client staff join through HR onboarding. Barrels
  superusers are invited only.
- **Superuser by invitation only:** a superuser who has passed two-step sign-in
  invites another by email. The invited person accepts, sets up an authenticator
  and saves recovery codes in one visit. The general account routes can no longer
  grant superuser status, removing it needs a two-step superuser session, every
  change is audited, and the last superuser cannot be removed.
- **Recovery codes without a password:** accounts that sign in with an emailed code
  can now create recovery codes too.
- **Safer clean-up:** removing someone from an organisation now removes every role
  that came with it, however many there are.

### Tickets

- **A ticket registry for the gate:** the API now holds each event's tickets and
  pairs gate phones with a secret that is stored only as a hash. A paired phone can
  check a ticket and admit it once. The scanner screens that use it come in the
  next release.

### Reliability

- **One failure, one alert:** a CAP alert with nothing to draw no longer fails its
  social card; it is skipped. A delivery job that fails for good raises its error
  once instead of every 15 minutes for a week.
