# organisations domain — agent context

**Owner:** Barrels platform (ADR-0022). Platform notion of "an organisation and who acts for it", independent of employment. Not a GAA/GMS module.

- Tables live in the `org` schema of the main database (main Alembic history, `org` is in `_TRACKED_SCHEMAS`): `org.organisation` (id, slug, name, kind `barrels|client|business`, status `active|suspended`, `verified_at`), `org.membership` (role `owner|admin|member`, status `active|invited|removed`, unique per organisation and user), `org.organisation_app` (entitlement per `src/auth/apps.py` app key).
- **Bridge A (ADR-0022):** `org.organisation.id` shares the keyspace of `hr.organisation.id` (`"gaa"`). HR's create path and the baseline seed write the platform row in the same transaction through `service.ensure_for_hr_organisation`. HR reads and foreign keys do not change. `service.reconcile_hr_organisations` / `missing_platform_organisations` detect and repair drift; the reconciliation test must stay green before Phase 2 (an HR foreign key).
- New organisations get immutable `org_<hex>` ids (`service.create_organisation`); the `slug` is the editable, unique, human-readable name.
- Service functions `flush` and never `commit`; the caller owns the transaction.
- **Request context:** `OrgContext` (`dependencies.py`) resolves the organisation from `X-Barrels-Organisation`. Missing header 400; unknown organisation or non-member 404 (same message, no existence leak); member with too low a role 403; suspended organisation 403. Superusers get no bypass: they act on organisations through a membership or the future control plane. A route is only "organisation-isolated" if it uses `OrgContext` **and** has a cross-organisation denial test.
- **Membership management (F4, `members.py`):** `org.invitation` (organisation, normalised email, role, SHA-256 `token_hash`, `expires_at` 7 days, `accepted_at`, `revoked_at`). Invitations are single-use, expiring and revocable; re-inviting an address revokes its pending invitation. The email goes through the `notifications` outbox (`organisation-invitation` template, transactional) and holds the only copy of the token. Accept takes the token in the body and requires the signed-in account's email to equal the invitation's. Admins manage members and admins; only owners invite owners or touch the owner role; the last active owner is protected (409). Removal sets `status = removed`; a later invitation reactivates the same row. Path `{organisation_id}` must equal the header organisation.
- **Audit:** `audit.py` registers organisation, membership, invitation (without `token_hash`) and app rows under the `organisation` history subject, readable by that organisation's admins and owners. Services set the audit actor.
- **Control plane (C1):** `GET /organisations` (paginated, `q`/`status`/`kind` filters, active member counts) and `GET /organisations/{id}` (adds app entitlements) are superuser-only (`AdminUser`) and read across organisations by design; they are the "future control plane" the request-context rule refers to. They serve `apps/web/admin`. Declared after `/mine` and `/current` so those literal paths win. 
- **Control plane writes (C2, `AdminUser`, committed by the router):** `POST /organisations` (kind `barrels|client|business`), `PATCH /organisations/{id}` (name, slug, kind, status active/suspended; suspending makes `OrgContext` answer 403), `POST /organisations/{id}/verify` and `/unverify` (reason required; 409 when already in that state), `PUT /organisations/{id}/apps/{app_key}` (422 for a key outside `src/auth/apps.py`), `GET /organisations/{id}/admin/members` (superuser roster; the organisation-scoped `/members` stays admin/owner only). Schemas are named `PlatformOrganisationCreate/Update` because HR already owns `OrganisationCreate/Update` in the generated client. Every write sets the audit actor and lands under `platform_organisation`; verify and unverify add an explicit entry (`action` verify/unverify, change `reason`) next to the `verified_at` field diff.
- **Verified organisers (C2):** an Events organiser linked to an organisation (F5) shows `verified` from `org.organisation.verified_at`; unlinked legacy organisers keep their own flag. `verification.organisation_is_verified` does the cross-database read through a 30 s in-process cache that verify/unverify clear on the worker that handled them (other workers catch up within the TTL).
- `dependencies.resolve_org_context(session, user, header)` holds the resolution and denial rules. `OrgContext` feeds it an `AccountUser`; `storage.dependencies.UploadMember` feeds it a user from either an account or an app-scoped (Events) token.
- Routes use `AccountUser` (any active account, staff-approved or not), not `CurrentUser`, because organisation members need not be staff.
- **Events bridge (F5):** `events.organisers.organisation_id` points at an organisation here; `service.list_managed_organisation_ids` feeds the Events organiser gate.
- **Public sites (PF1, ADR-0025 N1/N2, `sites.py`):** `org.public_site` (immutable `key`, owner, `site_url`, `contact_recipient`, `accepts_contact`/`accepts_subscriptions`, `turnstile_mode` inherit/off/enforce, `turnstile_site_key`, write-only `turnstile_secret_encrypted`). Public forms name a site by key; `resolve_public_site` treats a suspended owner as unknown. Null settings fall back to the old environment settings (`contact.config.recipient_for`, `SUBSCRIPTIONS_SITE_URLS`) until retired. Secrets are Fernet-encrypted with `ORG_SECRET_ENCRYPTION_KEYS` (`src/utils/secret_box.py`, shared with TOTP); APIs return only `turnstile_secret_set`, audit excludes the column, and a save without keys is 503. Routes: `GET /organisations/public/sites/{site_key}`, `GET /organisations/org/sites` (`OrgAdmin`), and the superuser-only `GET`/`POST /organisations/admin/sites` and `PATCH /organisations/admin/sites/{site_id}`. Defaults (`barrels`, `signal` → `barrels`; `mbia`, `gms-alerts` → `gaa`) come from migration `orgsites20261020` and `ensure_default_public_sites[_sync]` in the installation seed.
- **Entitlements (PF1, `entitlements.py`):** keys are registered apps (`src/auth/apps.py`) plus platform products (`PRODUCTS`: `bookings`, `rewards`). `require_org_app(key, minimum)` resolves `OrgContext`, checks the role, then requires an enabled `org.organisation_app` row (`OrganisationAppNotEntitledError`, 403, raised only after membership is proven). Bookings and rewards organisation routes use it; a new product adds its key to `PRODUCTS` and grants it in its seed or migration. Events is not gated yet, because legacy organisers have no organisation.
- Public schemas never expose ORM models.

## Tests
`tests/organisations/` — service and `/organisations/mine` (`test_organisations.py`), migration backfill and downgrade (`test_migration.py`), `OrgContext` denial and role tests (`test_context.py`), invitations, roles, last-owner, cross-organisation denial and audit (`test_members.py`). HR/baseline dual-write and reconciliation: `tests/organisations/test_hr_bridge.py`. Control-plane list/detail and their denial for non-superusers: `tests/organisations/test_admin_list.py`. Control-plane writes, audit rows, unknown app 422, suspension regression: `tests/organisations/test_control_plane_writes.py`; public sites: `test_public_sites.py` and `test_public_sites_migration.py`; entitlements: `test_entitlements.py`; verified organiser in Events: `tests/events/test_organiser_verification.py`.
