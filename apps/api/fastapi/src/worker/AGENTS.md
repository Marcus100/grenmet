# worker domain — agent context

**Owner:** Barrels platform runtime. ARQ worker on Redis.

- `main.py` `WorkerSettings`: functions and cron jobs — `process_cap_jobs`, `ingest_cap_feeds`, `send_notification_emails` (every 30 s), `send_outbound_emails` (every 30 s; legacy drain only, see the platform outbox note), `send_wis2_publications` (every 30 s; eRegister CSV transfer to wis2box, no-op unless `WIS2BOX_*` is set, retry state on `eregister_send`), `run_notification_sweeps` (10:00 daily), `send_invoice_reminders` (11:00 UTC daily = 07:00 Grenada; idempotent per invoice and stage, see `src/billing/AGENTS.md`), `poll_flights` (minutes 5 and 35; no-op unless `FLIGHTS_OPENSKY_ENABLED`, see `src/flights/AGENTS.md`), `fetch_marine_environment` (hours 1, 7, 13, 19 UTC at :20) and `fetch_air_environment` (10:30 and 22:30 UTC; ADS queue, 30 min job timeout); both no-op without Copernicus credentials, see `src/environmental/AGENTS.md`.
- `dispatch.py`: due-job processing with exponential retry (`_retry_delay_seconds`). `publishers.py`: CAP side effects (webhooks, PDF, social image, static map). `last_known_good.py` (job `publish.last_known_good`, queued with the other CAP side effects) rewrites `alerts/last-known-good.json` and `.html` in storage from the active public alerts for storm resilience; skips when storage is unconfigured. Runbook: `docs/operations/cloudflare-storm-cache.md`. `cap_email.py` (`publish.email`: one non-transactional outbox email per confirmed `gms-alerts` subscriber, idempotent on `outbound_email.dedupe_key`, suppression-aware) and `cap_push.py` (`publish.push`: Web Push, see `src/push/AGENTS.md`) are the CAP dissemination channels; `cap_content.py` builds their shared alert text. Both register in `dispatch.KIND_HANDLERS` and `cap/tasks.PUBLISH_JOB_KINDS`.
- `fetch_model_guidance` (hourly at `MODELS_GUIDANCE_POLL_MINUTE`, 3600 s timeout; no-op unless `MODELS_GUIDANCE_ENABLED`) stores new ECMWF/GFS point-series runs, see `src/models_guidance/AGENTS.md`.
- `dispatch.py`: due-job processing with exponential retry (`_retry_delay_seconds`). `publishers.py`: CAP side effects (webhooks, PDF, social image, static map).
- `check_nhc_failures` (every 5 min) reads failed `archive_nhc_jobs` read-only and notifies `nhc.pull_failed` (`wxwatch/alerts.py`); `check_alert_outbox` (every 15 min) fails when due emails sit unsent for 10 minutes so job metrics show the alert path is stuck.
- Dead letters (the platform outbox) call `operational_metrics.outbox_dead_lettered` after the commit: one error log `"Outbox message dead-lettered"` with `outbox`, `topic`, `outbox_id`, `attempts` (never payload or `last_error`) and a daily Redis counter `telemetry:v1:<env>:outbox:<date>` field `<outbox>|<topic>|dead`. `check_outbox_backlog` (minutes 2, 17, 32, 47; `outbox_health.py`) records gauges in `telemetry:v1:<env>:outbox-backlog` for dead rows in the last `OUTBOX_DEAD_WINDOW_HOURS` (24) and pending rows overdue by `OUTBOX_STALE_MINUTES` (30), and logs `"Outbox backlog above threshold"` at warning when either exceeds `OUTBOX_ALERT_THRESHOLD` (0). Tests: `tests/worker/test_outbox_health.py`, `tests/events/test_outbox.py`.
- Sentry Crons (`cron_monitor.py`): `@monitored` wraps `process_cap_jobs`, `ingest_cap_feeds`, `drain_outbox` and `purge_expired_data`; slug `<ENVIRONMENT>-worker-<job-name>`, monitor config upserted with each check-in. No-op unless Sentry is initialised and `SENTRY_CRONS_ENABLED=true` (Sentry cron monitors can be billable). Check-in failures never fail a job. Tests: `tests/worker/test_cron_monitor.py`.
- Rules: new jobs must be idempotent, retry-safe, logged, and monitored; persist job state in the database (like `CapJobEvent`) rather than relying on Redis alone.
- Health probes must load only settings and Redis dependencies; load task/database/image dependencies during startup or job execution.
- Tests: `tests/worker/` (`test_dispatch.py`, `test_last_known_good.py`, `test_health_import.py`).
- Related: `src/cap/AGENTS.md`, `src/notifications/AGENTS.md`.

- `nhc.py` owns queue `grenmet:nhc`. Its dispatcher drains persisted WxWatch pull/point requests only; never install automatic NHC collection. Preserve archive locking, conditional requests, bounded downloads, coalescing and recoverable leases.

- `purge_expired_data` (03:30 UTC daily, `retention.py`): deletes contact messages and data requests 24 months after receipt, pending subscriptions after 30 days, and nulls page-feedback comments after 24 months. Since PF2 it also deletes expired `platform.idempotency_key` rows (24 h), `done`/`dead` `platform.outbox` rows after 30 days, and clears `bookings.booking.manage_token_hash` 90 days after a booking ends (`PurgeResult.idempotency_keys`, `outbox_rows`, `booking_tokens`). Idempotent, batched, logs counts only. Policy table: docs/security.md, Data retention.
- Topic `webhook.deliver` (PF6, `src/webhooks_out/delivery.py`) is imported by `startup` and `drain_outbox`; see `src/webhooks_out/AGENTS.md`.
- **Platform outbox (PF2, ADR-0026, `src/outbox.py`, `platform.outbox`):** domains call `outbox.enqueue(session, topic=, payload=, dedupe_key=)` inside the transaction that causes the effect (unique `(topic, dedupe_key)`; no commit) and register `@outbox.handler("topic")`. Job `drain_outbox` (cron seconds 10 and 40) calls `outbox.drain`: one row per transaction, `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, the handler's writes and the row's `done` commit together, a failure rolls the handler back, backs off 60 s doubling to 1 h and the row is `dead` after 5 attempts (`last_error` holds only the exception type; `done` rows lose their payload). Rows whose topic has no handler stay pending. Handlers are registered by importing their module: add it to `startup` and to `drain_outbox`. Email runs entirely on this outbox (N4a): `email.send` (`src/notifications/outbox_handlers.py`, `enqueue_email`; the payload is the whole message, the handler sends it and logs an `OutboundEmail` row) and `email.deliver` (queued by `notifications.service.enqueue_email`/`enqueue_email_once`; payload is only the `OutboundEmail` id, the row holds the content and is the delivery log). Retry/backoff/dead-lettering is the outbox's (60 s doubling to 1 h, dead after 5); `outbox.handler(topic, on_dead=...)` lets a read model record the give-up. Legacy drain: `send_outbound_emails` still sends `OutboundEmail` rows that have no `email.deliver` outbox row (queued before N4a, or by an older API during a deploy) and ignores the rest. Remove that cron job, `dispatch.process_outbound_emails` and `_deliver_outbound` once `select count(*) from outbound_email o where status in ('pending','failed') and attempts < 5 and not exists (select 1 from platform.outbox b where b.topic='email.deliver' and b.dedupe_key = o.id::text)` stays 0 after a deploy. `enqueue_booking_reminders` (hourly at :07) is the first scheduled producer. `expire_unpaid_bookings` (every 5 minutes, minutes 2, 7, ...) cancels unpaid deposit bookings past `BOOKINGS_DEPOSIT_HOLD_MINUTES` and releases their slots (`bookings.deposits.expire_unpaid`); `src.bookings.outbox_handlers` (topic `bookings.deposit_paid`) is imported in `startup` and `drain_outbox`. Events paid tiers (N4b): `expire_unpaid_event_orders` (every 5 minutes, minutes 3, 8, ...; no-op without `EVENTS_DATABASE_URL`) cancels unpaid orders past `EVENTS_ORDER_HOLD_MINUTES` and releases their stock (`events.order_fulfilment.expire_unpaid`); `src.events.payment_handlers` (topics `events.order_paid`, `events.order_refunded`) is imported in `startup` and `drain_outbox`, runs on the main session and opens its own Events session (see `src/events/AGENTS.md`). Existing loops (Events, notification deliveries, CAP, eRegister) are not moved onto it. Idempotency keys for requests live in `src/idempotency.py` (`run_idempotent`, `Idempotency-Key` dependency). Tests: `tests/test_outbox.py`, `tests/test_idempotency.py`, `tests/worker/test_platform_jobs.py`.
